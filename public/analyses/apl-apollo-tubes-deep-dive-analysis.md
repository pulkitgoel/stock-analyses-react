## 2. Business Overview

APL Apollo is **India's leading branded steel tubes manufacturer** with 10 manufacturing facilities, 1,500+ product varieties and over 5,000 SKUs. Products serve MS Black Pipes, Galvanised Tubes, Pre-Galvanised Tubes, Structural ERW Steel Tubes and Hollow Sections, used across housing, urban infrastructure, irrigation, solar plants, greenhouses and engineering applications.

### Core Strengths

- **~60–65% market share in structural tubes** — dominant scale advantage over competitors
- **65% of sales from value-added products** (structural hollow sections, premium brands), targeting 75–80% to reduce steel-price volatility exposure
- **Multi-brand portfolio:** Apollo Structural (DFT technology), Patra, SG Premium (vs secondary steel), SUKIRU (solar infrastructure)
- **January 2025 pricing repositioning** — branded products carry a ₹500/ton premium, driving margin expansion

### Competitive Moat

- Largest branded steel tube player with national distribution network across India
- Pricing power evidenced by EBITDA/ton +18% YoY (₹5,522) even during weak volumes
- Value-added mix expansion structurally reduces exposure to raw-material commoditization
- Net cash position funds aggressive capex without equity dilution
