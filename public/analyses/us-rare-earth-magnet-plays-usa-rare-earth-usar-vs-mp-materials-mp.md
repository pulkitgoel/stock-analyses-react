## 3. Business Overview

### 🇺🇸 USA Rare Earth (USAR) — The Government-Backed Mine-to-Magnet Pure Play
USAR is building one of the most vertically integrated rare-earth platforms in the Western world:
- **Round Top (Texas)** — one of the largest rare-earth deposits in the US (heavy + light rare earths).
- **Stillwater, Oklahoma magnet plant** — Phase 1a commissioned Mar 2026 (600 MTPA by Q4'26 → 1,200 MTPA Q1'27). Produces sintered NdFeB permanent magnets.
- **LCM (UK)** — metal & alloy capacity expanding to 3,000 MTPA.
- **Serra Verde (Brazil)** — $2.8B acquisition of the **only scaled producer of all 4 magnetic rare earths outside Asia**; closes end Aug 2026.
- **Carester (Europe)** — strategic rare-earth partnership (signed Jul 23, 2026).

**Key drivers:** $1.5B PIPE (Jan 2026) + $1.6B DoC CHIPS funding (10%+ govt equity stake) = **$1.75B cash** to deploy. Phases 1a/1b magnet ramp. Essentially **pre-revenue** today.

### 🇺🇸 MP Materials (MP) — The Larger, Revenue-Generating Producer
MP is the **largest US rare-earth producer** (Mountain Pass, CA) executing a mine-to-magnet strategy:
- **Materials segment:** NdPr oxide/metal sales + PPA income (price-protection agreement with DoD).
- **Magnetics segment:** Independence (Fort Worth, TX) magnetization; commercial magnet shipments begin **Q4 2026**.
- **10X magnet campus** construction accelerating.
- **Heavy rare earths:** first gadolinium long-term offtake with a US aerospace/defense customer.
- **Project Swarm:** demand-aggregation for drone magnets (subscriptions with US/allied customers).
- **Key contracts:** $400M Pentagon investment + $150M loan, $500M Apple partnership, GM offtake.
