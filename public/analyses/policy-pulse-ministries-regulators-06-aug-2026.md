### 🏛️ SEBI Buy-Back Open-Market Route — Live Since Aug 1, With Tighter Caps

**What happened:** The **SEBI (Buy-Back of Securities) (Amendment) Regulations 2026** (notified Jul 1, **effective Aug 1**) **restored the open-market buy-back route** through the stock exchange (discontinued Apr 1, 2025) alongside the tender-offer and book-building routes. New safeguards: **open-market buy-backs capped at <15% of paid-up capital + free reserves**, stricter promoter-holding and public-shareholding norms, and merchant-banker representation made optional. This **reopens a capital-return mechanism** for cash-rich listed companies.

|-------|--------|--------|
| TCS | 🟢 Positive | Cash-rich IT leader — open-market buy-back route restored |
| INFY | 🟢 Positive | High cash generation; buy-back flexibility helps support price |
| ITC | 🟢 Positive | Large cash balance — buy-back route re-opens a return lever |
| HDFCBANK | 🟡 Neutral | Capital-return tool available but bank capital rules dominate |
| COALINDIA | 🟡 Neutral | PSU with buy-back history; route restored, timing uncertain |
| ONGC | 🟡 Neutral | Cash-rich PSU; buy-back flexibility is a mild support |

**Key signal:** Fresh tool for **capital return** — structurally supportive for **consistently cash-generative names** prone to buy-backs (ITC, TCS, INFY). The <15% cap and promoter-freeze rules keep it investor-protective (a mild constraint versus pre-2025).
