## Trump's August Trade Blotter, and the "Buy a Dell" Contradiction on the Same Day He Handed Dell a Medal

President Trump's **August financial disclosure**, signed **September 17** and made public on **Thursday, October 8**, logged **517 securities transactions** worth roughly **USD 74.3 million to USD 273.3 million**, according to CNBC's tally of the 18-page filing. Purchases accounted for at least **USD 44.2 million**, sales at least **USD 30.1 million**. The **Dell** line is the one drawing attention.

- The independently managed accounts **sold USD 500,001-1,000,000 of Dell on August 21**, and a further **USD 15,001-50,000 on August 28** — months after Trump told a crowd in Rome, Georgia on **February 19** to "go out and buy a Dell computer." The accounts had **bought USD 1-5 million of Dell on February 10**, when the stock traded near USD 126.
- **Nvidia** was bought **USD 250,001-500,000 on August 3** and **USD 500,001-1,000,000 on August 17**, then **sold USD 500,001-1,000,000 on August 21** — the same day Trump awarded Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang the **National Medal of Science**.
- The single largest line of the month was a **USD 5-25 million purchase of Meta Platforms on August 21**. On the same reshuffle day the accounts bought **USD 1-5 million each of AT&T, ConocoPhillips, Abbott Laboratories, Netflix and Chevron**, and **sold USD 1-5 million each of Advanced Micro Devices and Church & Dwight**, plus **USD 500,001-1,000,000 positions in Boeing, Home Depot, T-Mobile, Datadog, Dell, Palo Alto Networks and Nvidia**.
- On **August 10** the portfolio added **USD 1-5 million each of Microsoft, McDonald's and Comcast**; on **August 17** it bought **USD 250,001-500,000 of Amazon**. Two crypto-linked trades on **August 10**: bought **USD 1,001-15,000 of Strategy**, sold **USD 1,001-15,000 of Coinbase**. An **Intel** sale of **USD 100,000-250,000** landed as the government holds a **10% stake** in the company.
- On **August 18** the accounts bought **USD 1-5 million of SpaceX senior unsecured notes** (5.35%, maturing July 2031) — **two days before** Trump signed a national space transportation policy targeting **more than 1,000 launches and reentries a year by 2030**.
- The White House says Trump has **"no ability"** to influence the trades and that they run through discretionary, index-replicating models. The **2025 annual disclosure** showed **more than 21,000 trades across eight accounts** holding at least **USD 858 million**.

**The signal is the dating, not the size.** On **October 8** — the same day the disclosure was published — Trump presented the National Medal of Science and Technology to **Elon Musk (SpaceX/Tesla), Sergey Brin (Google), Jensen Huang (Nvidia), Lisa Su (AMD), Michael Dell (Dell) and Satya Nadella (Microsoft)** at a Washington summit. The portfolio he says he cannot direct was, two months earlier, selling and buying the same names whose founders were still being decorated.

## Washington Froze Microsoft and Adobe Out of the Green-Card Program

Vice President **JD Vance** and Labor Secretary **Keith Sonderling** announced on **October 8** that the Department of Labor is suspending **Microsoft** and **Adobe**, plus several large IT outsourcing firms, from the **Permanent Labor Certification (PERM)** programme — the main federal route employers use to sponsor foreign workers for employment-based green cards. The administration also opened probes into **nine top universities**.

- Sonderling said new filings will be refused and pending ones halted for the named companies; he cited **almost 3 million foreign-worker requests since 2009**, **over 230,000 H-1B approvals** and **more than 100,000 permanent labor certifications** across them.
- Vance singled out **Microsoft**, saying it laid off **6,000 American workers** in 2025 while using about **6,300 H-1B visas** and nearly **3,000 green cards**. Microsoft pushed back, saying about **80% of its roughly 6,000 H-1B filings last year were extensions or status changes for existing employees**, not new hires.
- The move sits inside **"Project Firewall,"** an enforcement drive with roughly **200 open cases** and a **USD 100,000 fee** on fresh H-1B petitions for workers hired from abroad. PERM certifications fell to **104,028** this fiscal year from **138,093** a year earlier.

*This is a US-policy item; the read-through for Indian-listed IT services firms is covered in today's Modi and Ministries editions.*

## SpaceX Buys Nationwide 800 MHz Spectrum — Telecoms Sold Off and Tower REITs Rose

**SpaceX** agreed on **October 8** to acquire **Grain Management's nationwide 800 MHz spectrum portfolio** — up to **14 megahertz** of paired low-band airwaves — in a deal The Wall Street Journal reported at about **USD 8 billion**, subject to FCC approval. The spectrum was originally **T-Mobile's**, sold to Grain in August 2026.

- US and European telecoms tumbled. **T-Mobile, Verizon and AT&T fell between 5.5% and 7.4%** in premarket trade, and the **STOXX Europe 600 Telecommunications** index dropped more than **2%** to its lowest since January 30.
- **Morgan Stanley** called the deal "a clear sign that SpaceX is going to be a more aggressive acquirer of spectrum," while cautioning that any threat to the carriers "is likely to materialize gradually, starting in rural markets."
- The transaction stacks on SpaceX's earlier **EchoStar** spectrum purchases (initially valued near **USD 17 billion**, later amended) and an FCC grant on **October 6** to deploy **15,000 direct-to-cell satellites**.
- The three largest US tower REITs initially rose **3-4.5%** in after-hours trade on the reading that satellite-to-phone connectivity still needs ground infrastructure, even as it bypasses carrier-owned radio access.

## "Super Intelligence": Anyone Saying "Artificial Intelligence" Is Now, in Trump's Words, "THE ENEMY"

Trump posted on **Truth Social** that the White House "considers anyone that uses the term 'Artificial Intelligence,' as opposed to the highly accepted new and more accurate term, 'Super Intelligence,' THE ENEMY!" It builds on **Executive Order 14434**, "Inaugurating the Era of Super Intelligence," signed **September 29**, which orders every federal department and agency to replace "AI" with "SI" in correspondence, websites, reports and policy documents.

- The rename carries the **same legal definition** as AI in statute (15 U.S.C. § 9401); previously issued rules, contracts and historical documents are exempt. The president's science adviser has until about **November 28** to propose legislative language for a federal definition.
- On the sidelines, six executives signed a "White House Accord on Super Intelligence": **Sundar Pichai (Google), Elon Musk (xAI/SpaceX), Dario Amodei (Anthropic), Mark Zuckerberg (Meta), Greg Brockman (OpenAI) and Jensen Huang (Nvidia)**.
- More than **USD 5 billion** has been allocated to the **Genesis Mission** applying "SI" to science, medicine, energy and manufacturing — the funding is the substance; the acronym is the signal.
