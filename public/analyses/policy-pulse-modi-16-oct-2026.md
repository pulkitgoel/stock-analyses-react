## The PM's 2036 Olympics Ambition, Priced the Next Morning at ₹7,386.74 Crore

Prime Minister **Narendra Modi** met India's Asian Games medallists at the Major Dhyan Chand Stadium in New Delhi on **October 15** and used the occasion to restate the hosting ambition: "India is emerging in establishing itself as a credible and hospitable country for international sports events. We are bidding for 2036 Olympics and it is our resolve that we will host the games." He also referenced the **2030 Commonwealth Games** bid.

India finished the Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games with **85 medals, 21 of them gold**, and **38 of the medallists were women, including 11 gold medallists**.

The follow-through arrived within a day. On **October 16** the **Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports** issued the revised operational guidelines for the **Scheme of Assistance to National Sports Federations**, continued for **2026-27 to 2030-31** against the 16th Finance Commission cycle, with a total outlay of **₹7,386.74 crore**. The material changes:

- **Financial assistance to host an international event in India rises from ₹2 crore to up to ₹50 crore**, scaled by the nature and significance of the event and the number of participating countries and athletes.
- **Up to ₹50 crore** is available for conducting the **National Games**, covering sports equipment, timing/scoring/results services and technical officials' remuneration.
- The scheme formally proposes **Olympic Training Centres (OTCs)** dedicated to individual sports, with sports-science equipment and expert personnel, explicitly to **reduce athletes' reliance on overseas training**.
- The scheme continues to fund national coaching camps, foreign coaches, high performance directors, physiotherapists, sports scientists, overseas exposure and grassroots development.

## Rubio Lands in Jamnagar Two Days After the Graham Act Deadline

The United States Secretary of State **Marco Rubio** begins a visit to India on **October 17** and stays until **October 22**, with stops in **Mumbai and Jamnagar** — his second official visit this year. The State Department media note is explicit that he will "discuss prospects for **enhanced energy cooperation**" and "meet with business leaders to promote opportunities for commercial investment".

**Jamnagar** is the significant detail. It is home to **Reliance Industries' refining complex, the world's largest single-site refining facility, with capacity to process around 1.4 million barrels of crude per day.** The State Department did not explain the choice of city.

The timing is what makes this a trade-relevant item rather than a diplomatic one:

- The India stop **falls days after the October 18 Graham Act deadline** on duties of up to 100% for the five largest buyers of Russian energy.
- Negotiations on the **India-US Bilateral Trade Agreement** have stalled again, with New Delhi objecting to legislation that could carry that 100% tariff.
- India **briefly reduced Russian energy imports earlier in 2026 and purchases have since risen**, as Iran-war supply disruptions pushed it back into discounted barrels.
- US Ambassador to India **Sergio Gor** has said Prime Minister Modi and President Trump spoke recently, adding to "stay tuned for what comes next."

## Tamil Nadu Tops Up the Union Semiconductor Incentive

On **October 15**, Tamil Nadu Chief Minister **C. Joseph Vijay** launched the **Tamil Nadu Semiconductor Ecosystem Scheme 2026** at the Secretariat in Chennai, designed to add state incentives **on top of** central assistance rather than instead of it.

- **Category one** provides additional incentives and support to projects approved under the Centre's **Semicon India Programme 2.0 / India Semiconductor Mission**, with no ceiling disclosed.
- **Category two** offers customised, need-based incentives to projects **outside** the central scheme that are considered strategically important to the state's ecosystem.
- Coverage is the full value chain: wafer fabrication, raw materials, assembly, testing, packaging and supply.
- It follows the Union government's approval of **Semicon India Programme 2.0 on July 15, 2026** and the state's August announcement of a **Semiconductor and Electronics Park at Madhuramangalam in Kancheepuram district**.
