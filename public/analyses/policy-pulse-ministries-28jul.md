## 🏛️ SEBI's Sweeping PMS Overhaul: MF-Only Category Opens Retail Wealth Door

SEBI's July consultation paper on Portfolio Management Services (PMS) continues to generate market interest. The proposed Mutual Fund-only PMS (MF-PMS) category — with a ₹25 lakh minimum investment (down from ₹50 lakh) — would democratize access to professional wealth management for India's underserved affluent retail investors. Portfolio managers would invest only in direct plans of mutual funds, ETFs, and SIFs.
