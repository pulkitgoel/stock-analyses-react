# Policy Pulse — Ministries/Regulators — 18 Aug 2026

## 🇮🇳 Ministry of Defence: Rs 1,943 Crore MQ-9B Sea Guardian Drone Lease Contract

The Indian Ministry of Defence has signed a **₹1,943-crore contract** with **General Atomics Aeronautical Systems** to lease two advanced **MQ-9B Sea Guardian drones** for a **30-month period**, finalized on **August 17, 2026**, in New Delhi.

### Contract Details
- **Value**: ₹1,943 crore ($232 million approx)
- **Duration**: 30 months (interim arrangement)
- **Vendor**: General Atomics Aeronautical Systems (U.S.)
- **Purpose**: Immediate fortification of Indian Navy's maritime surveillance and intelligence-gathering capabilities across the Indian Ocean Region

### Strategic Context
This is an **interim acquisition** to ensure no operational gap while India awaits delivery of **31 permanently purchased MQ-9B aircraft** — a comprehensive defense procurement deal sealed earlier. The Indian Navy currently operates two MQ-9A models under a previous lease initiated during border tensions in 2020.
