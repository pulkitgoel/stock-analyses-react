## GOBARdhan Goes Live: India's ₹23,731 Crore Compressed Biogas Scheme

**The Union government operationalised GOBARdhan — the National Unified Scheme for Compressed Biogas — on October 1, 2026**, with Petroleum and Natural Gas Minister **Hardeep Singh Puri** launching the Central Sector Scheme in New Delhi alongside a GOBARdhan Handbook. The scheme was **approved by the Union Cabinet chaired by Prime Minister Narendra Modi on August 6, 2026**.

The numbers:

- **Outlay: ₹23,731 crore**, implemented over **FY 2026-27 to FY 2035-36** — a ten-year, policy-visible runway.
- **The target: domestic CBG output raised nearly tenfold, to about 5 million metric standard cubic metres per day (MMSCMD).**
- **An administered CBG price of ₹2,110 per MMBTU**, designed to give producers a stable, bankable revenue line rather than exposure to spot gas.
- **Assured offtake** for CBG into the city-gas and industrial networks, plus **capital assistance, pipeline connectivity and credit support**.
- **Consolidation:** the CBG ecosystem was previously spread across four ministries; GOBARdhan brings it under a single administrative frame.
- **Feedstock** is agricultural residue, cattle dung, press mud, municipal organic waste and other biomass — which is why the scheme straddles energy, agriculture and rural income rather than sitting in one silo.
- **Stated co-benefits:** more than **₹75,000 crore** of GDP contribution, over **150,000 jobs**, around **250 million tonnes of organic fertiliser** production and a reduction of more than **40 million tonnes of CO2**.

Why this matters beyond the headline outlay: the binding constraint on India's CBG sector has never been technology or feedstock — it has been **offtake certainty and price visibility**, which is precisely what a fixed administered price plus assured network access addresses. That is the difference between a subsidy and an investable market structure.

## People's Plan Campaign 2026-27 Launches on Gandhi Jayanti

**The People's Plan Campaign (PPC) 2026-27, themed "Sabki Yojana, Sabka Vikas," begins across India on October 2, 2026** — deliberately launched on Gandhi Jayanti. The campaign kicks off the process by which **Gram Panchayats prepare their Gram Panchayat Development Plans (GPDPs) for the financial year 2027-28**.

This is a process story, not a spending announcement, and it should be read as such. But the PPC is the mechanism through which the bottom-up rural capital-expenditure pipeline is set for the following fiscal — which is why it effectively opens the rural-demand narrative for FY28.

## Putin Praises Modi's Ukraine Peace Effort

**Russian President Vladimir Putin publicly praised PM Modi's "good ideas" for a peaceful resolution of the Ukraine conflict**, saying India has been actively pushing for mutually acceptable solutions: *"We are thankful for his desire to put an end to this conflict through peaceful means and as soon as possible."*

For Indian equities, the material channel is crude. Russia is a sanctioned supplier whose barrels have flowed to Indian refiners at a discount through the conflict; any credible de-escalation path changes both the discount and the sanctions-overhang calculus that Washington has linked to tariffs on Indian goods — the same US law that could impose tariffs of up to 100% on India over Russian oil imports. This is a long-horizon signal, not a trade.
