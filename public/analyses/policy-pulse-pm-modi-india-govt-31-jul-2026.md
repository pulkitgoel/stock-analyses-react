# Policy Pulse — PM Modi / India Govt — 31 Jul 2026

## 🛢️ Samudra Manthan — ₹84,084 Crore National Offshore Exploration Scheme (BIG DAY)

The Union Cabinet chaired by PM Narendra Modi approved **'Samudra Manthan'** — the National Offshore Exploration Scheme, a Central Sector Scheme of the Ministry of Petroleum & Natural Gas with an outlay of **₹84,084 crore** implemented up to FY 2030-31. This is one of the government's biggest-ever investments in offshore oil & gas exploration.

**What it covers:** large-scale seismic data acquisition/processing/interpretation, accelerated exploratory drilling in **deepwater and ultra-deepwater** areas, and scientific drilling in frontier basins. The goal is to boost domestic oil & gas production and slash import dependence amid the Hormuz/energy-security crisis.

## ☀️ PM-SSY (Surya Sarovar Yojana) — ₹5,070 Cr Floating Solar + Storage

The Cabinet approved the **Pradhan Mantri Surya Sarovar Yojana (PM-SSY)**: **5,000 MW** of Floating Solar Photovoltaic (FSPV) projects with co-located **Energy Storage Systems** (10,000 MWh storage, ≥2 hrs). India currently has only ~700 MW of floating solar — this is a ~7x capacity expansion push.
