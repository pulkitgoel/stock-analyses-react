## Assam Cabinet Adopts 75 Resolutions for the PM's 25 Years — and Reels Off the Northeast Project Book

The **Assam Cabinet** on **October 9** adopted **75 resolutions** recognising Prime Minister Narendra Modi's **25 years in public life**, and in doing so restated the state's largest industrial and infrastructure commitments. **PIB and the state government name projects and rupee values, not listed companies** — every company mapping below is an inference built from the named projects and known ownership, not a disclosed order.

- A **Rs 27,000-crore semiconductor assembly-and-test (ATMP) facility at Jagiroad** — **Tata Electronics'** plant, the first of its kind in India, which Chief Minister Himanta Biswa Sarma said will be **operational by the end of this year**, with the surrounding township named **Ratan Tata Electronics City**.
- A **Rs 28,000-crore expansion of the Numaligarh Refinery**.
- A proposed **Gohpur-Numaligarh twin-tube road tunnel** estimated at **Rs 18,662 crore**.
- A proposed **Namrup-IV ammonia-urea complex**, a **bamboo-based second-generation bioethanol plant at Numaligarh**, and the **718-km Barauni-Guwahati natural gas pipeline**.
- Separately, on Ratan Tata's death anniversary, Sarma credited the **Assam Cancer Care Foundation** — which mapped **17 hospitals** across the state — alongside the semiconductor plant as the Tata group's legacy in the Northeast.

## PM Modi Closes the Women and Child Development Summit: Nutrition, Anganwadi Reform, Child Protection

Prime Minister Modi addressed the **valedictory session of the Departmental Summit on Women and Child Development** at **Sushma Swaraj Bhawan on October 9 at about 4:30 pm**. The two-day summit (October 8-9) gathered Union and state leadership around **four themes**: reimagining **Anganwadi Centres** for better nutrition and early education; strengthening protection systems for vulnerable children; women's safety through institutional and legislative frameworks; and improving scheme outcomes for women's empowerment.

- Discussions covered the quality of **take-home rations and hot-cooked meals**, the accuracy of **Poshan Tracker** data, early childhood care and education, and child-protection mechanisms.
