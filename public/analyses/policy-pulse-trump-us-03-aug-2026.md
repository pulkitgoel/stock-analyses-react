# Policy Pulse — Trump / US — 03 Aug 2026

## 🇺🇸 Trump Cancels Iran Strike, Opens Talks — Oil Crashes, Energy Selloff, Cyclicals Rally

Today the defining US market catalyst is **geopolitical de-escalation**. President Trump called off a planned military strike on Iran and announced negotiations to begin Monday (Aug 3), seeking a deal to reopen the **Strait of Hormuz**. Oil prices collapsed ~5-7% (Brent below $84, WTI under $80) as a 60-day OFAC Iran oil license floods the market with crude during talks. Lower fuel costs and inflation relief lifted US equities and bonds.
