## Semicon 2.0 Six-Pillar Notifications Issued; SEMICON India 2026 Looms Sep 16-17

MeitY issued notifications for all six pillars of the Semicon 2.0 scheme (Sep 1), formally opening the application window for the Rs 1.27 lakh crore programme: (1) Design - semiconductor IPs/chips/SoCs for strategic and commercial use, including a deployment-linked incentive (DLI); (2) Machines and materials; (3) Fabs - silicon wafer fabs, compound semiconductor/photonics/sensors (incl. MEMS) fabs, and display fabs; (4) ATMP/OSAT packaging; (5) R&D for advanced technologies; (6) Talent development. Projects run up to six years; the scheme is open for applications for three years; ISM is the nodal agency (with C-DAC supporting design categories); semiconductor deep-tech awards start 2026; mid-term assessment after three years.

Minister's datapoints: 85,000 semiconductor engineers trained in four years (against a 10-year target); a fresh target of one lakh more; ~10% of the global semiconductor market expected in India; 250+ chips designed from Tier-2/Tier-3 cities; approvals granted in 90-200 days; 50,000-60,000 direct jobs expected.

## Power Ministry Allows Sale of Curtailed Renewable Power via Co-Located BESS

The Ministry of Power (Sep 2) permitted renewable generators to sell curtailed power through co-located battery energy storage systems - monetizing energy that would otherwise be spilled and improving plant economics. This nudges more storage capex alongside RE capacity and supports the storage-linked procurement theme (PM-SSY floating solar + storage was a Jul 31 cabinet item).
