## India-Belgium Summit: 10 Defence-Industry Pacts Name L&T and Bharat Forge's Kalyani

PM Modi hosted Belgian PM Bart De Wever (Sep 2-4) - the first Belgian prime minister visit to India in two decades, accompanied by Belgium's Defence and Foreign Trade Minister Theo Francken. The outcome is unusually concrete for a bilateral visit:

- **10 company-to-company defence-industrial agreements** covering naval mine-countermeasure systems, ammunition production lines, 70mm rockets, Zorawar light-tank turrets, electro-optics, loitering munitions, counter-drone systems and marine diesel engines - exchanged at an India-Belgium Defence CEOs Roundtable
- A **defence ministerial Letter of Intent** (staff talks, exercises, R&D, maritime security, emerging tech)
- Target to **double bilateral trade in five years**, with an Investment Fast-Track Mechanism
- Cooperation agreed in critical minerals, clean energy, semiconductors and maritime infrastructure; Belgium assured India it will extend no military technology to Pakistan
- PM Modi: the defence-industry agreements will "open up new opportunities for defence co-development and co-production"

**Named Indian industry links (from media coverage of the roundtable - the PIB political statement itself names no companies):**
- **L&T with Exail**: autonomous naval mine-countermeasure (MCM) systems - the Navy's long-delayed MCM vessel programme (12 vessels initially, requirement potentially 59; India has operated no minesweeper since 2019)
- **L&T with John Cockerill Defense**: turrets for the DRDO-L&T Zorawar light tank (Cockerill's 3105 turret is on the prototypes; Army eventual requirement 354 tanks; a Cockerill-electro-pneumatics JV will make 59 turrets at Chakan)
- **Kalyani Strategic Systems (Bharat Forge subsidiary) with Thales Belgium**: 70mm rocket assembly in India - first fully India-manufactured rocket expected early 2027; separately exploring a joint production centre with FN Herstal covering small arms and counter-drone equipment
- Other agreements (mostly with unlisted Indian partners): Lachaussee-Tembo ammunition line (~100M rounds/yr, EUR 50M, 2028-29), Sol1-DGVK loitering munitions/counter-drone, OIP Sensor-India Optel electro-optics for Arjun Mk II

## Trade and Sector Watch

The doubling-trade target with an Investment Fast-Track Mechanism is a framing commitment rather than a signed concession - watch for follow-through (Belgium is a key EU logistics/port hub and the diamond trade link runs through Antwerp). Critical minerals and semiconductor cooperation were agreed at framework level; no named Indian company or allocation yet. The strategic signal for Indian markets: European defence firms are lining up to co-produce in India as New Delhi expands domestic manufacturing - the same theme that has underpinned the MoD order flow (BEL, Zen Technologies, Tata Advanced Systems) all year.
