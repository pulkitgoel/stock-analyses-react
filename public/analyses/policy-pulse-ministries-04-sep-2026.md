## SEBI to Review Derivatives Settlement Pricing After CAS Volatility (PR 53/2026)

SEBI announced (PR 53/2026, Sep 3) it will review the methodology for determining **derivative contract settlement prices** following market feedback on the Closing Auction Session (CAS), live since Aug 3. Traders reported sudden option-price swings during the closing-auction window on expiry days, and the independently-run NSE/BSE closing auctions have produced settlement-price divergence. SEBI will issue a consultation paper on proposed changes within about a week.

**Market read:** capital-market stocks rallied Friday on the review — BSE rose ~3% intraday — because a cleaner, less volatile settlement mechanism removes an expiry-day friction and supports derivatives volumes (and brokerage order flow) into the September series.
