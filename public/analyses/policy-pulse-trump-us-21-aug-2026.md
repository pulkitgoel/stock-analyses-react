## US-Canada Trade Deal Deadline Looms (Section 338 Tariffs)

President Trump's 50% tariffs on $20 billion of Canadian goods under Section 338 of the Tariff Act of 1930 are set to take effect at 12:01 a.m. ET on Saturday, August 22, 2026, unless a final trade agreement is reached. Canadian Trade Minister Dominic LeBlanc stated on August 21 that negotiations are "very close" after extended meetings with USTR Jamieson Greer in Washington.

**Key Deal Terms Under Discussion:**
- Canadian auto tariffs could fall from 25% to 15%
- Steel and aluminum tariffs potentially reduced to 25% from current levels
- Canadian retaliatory tariffs on US goods may be removed
- The 50% Section 338 tariffs on alcohol, dairy, and non-automotive products would be averted

This marks the first use of Section 338 (a 1930 Great Depression-era law) in approximately 100 years. The tentative deal was announced by Trump on Truth Social on August 19, with a 3-day pause granted to finalize documentation.
