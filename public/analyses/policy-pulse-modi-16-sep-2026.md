## The Prime Minister Chairs the Semiconductor CEO Roundtable — SEMICON India Opens Tomorrow

**Prime Minister Narendra Modi chaired the Semiconductor Roundtable with leading CEOs at Seva Teerth today, September 16** (PIB release PRID 2311054, issued 7:44 pm). It is his annual closed-door session with global chip industry leadership, held deliberately the day before he opens India's flagship semiconductor event.

**PM Modi inaugurates SEMICON India 2026 tomorrow, September 17, at around 10 am at Yashobhoomi (India International Convention & Expo Centre), New Delhi** — the fifth edition, running **September 17-19** under the theme **"Silicon to Systems: Building the Ecosystem."** India is now the second-largest country delegation base in its own event. The scale numbers:

- **575-600+ participating companies, of which about 300 are international**
- **Participants from 52 countries; six country pavilions**
- **1,750+ booths, 150+ speakers, 400+ executives, expected footfall around 50,000**
- Organised jointly by the **India Semiconductor Mission (ISM), MeitY and SEMI**
- **Chip design tools have gone to 400+ colleges and 105 startups**; six country roundtables are built specifically for matchmaking and joint ventures

The policy stack behind it: **12 approved semiconductor manufacturing projects**, **Semicon 2.0 notified at ₹1,27,500 crore** (the Cabinet's second phase of the mission), four plants going commercial in 2026, and a stated target of training **1 lakh additional engineers**. A dedicated **Semicon 2.0 policy session is on the programme** — the most likely venue for the first signals on how the new co-investment and ecosystem incentives will actually be applied.

## Cabinet Hikes the EPFO Wage Ceiling From ₹15,000 to ₹25,000 — Effective Tomorrow

The Union Cabinet, chaired by the Prime Minister, approved the Ministry of Labour & Employment's proposal to raise the **mandatory EPFO wage ceiling from ₹15,000 to ₹25,000 per month** — **the first revision in 12 years**, and effective **from Vishwakarma Day, September 17, 2026**.

The numbers:

- **More than 51 lakh additional employees** come under mandatory EPFO coverage
- **Annual government outgo rises to about ₹11,339 crore** against existing budgetary support of about ₹10,250 crore
- **Estimated five-year expenditure: approximately ₹56,696 crore**
- The proposal was recommended by the **Expenditure Finance Committee on June 16, 2026** after inter-ministerial consultation
- Coverage expands across **provident fund savings, EPS pension protection and EDLI insurance**

The ceiling sat at ₹6,500 from 2004 to 2014 and was raised to ₹15,000 in September 2014. This is a formalisation measure first and a market-structure measure second — and it cuts both ways.

## India-New Zealand FTA Clears Its Final Legislative Hurdle — In Force October 19

**New Zealand's Parliament passed the implementing legislation 93-29 on September 16**, with opposition Labour support, clearing the last ratification step. The **India-New Zealand FTA takes effect October 19, 2026**.

- **95% of New Zealand's exports to India** will be eliminated or significantly reduced in tariff terms — and **57% become duty-free from day one**
- India gets **duty-free access to New Zealand for 100% of its exports**
- New Zealand commits to **USD 20 billion of investment in India over 15 years**
- **India retained protections for dairy and several agricultural products** — the sensitive-sector carve-out held
- Two-way trade was about **NZ$3.99 billion (USD 2.29 billion)** in the year to June 2026; India is New Zealand's ninth-largest goods and services export market
- Indian export sectors cited as beneficiaries: **textiles, leather, footwear, engineering goods, processed foods, ceramics, carpets and automobiles**

The Prime Minister hailed it as a landmark deal that will benefit farmers, youth and MSMEs. Commerce and Industry Minister Piyush Goyal has said India is negotiating FTAs with 8-9 more blocs and aims to cover 75% of global trade — this is the template: goods access abroad, sensitive agriculture protected at home.
