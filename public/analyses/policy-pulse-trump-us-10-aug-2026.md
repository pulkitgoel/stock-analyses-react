# Policy Pulse — Trump / US — 10 Aug 2026

## 🇺🇸 Trump's Historic Mining Roundtable Pumps $3B Into Rare Earnings, Magnets, and Battery Supply Chains — US Critical-Mineral Complex Rallies

On **Aug 7, 2026**, President Donald Trump convened the largest gathering of mining industry leaders at the White House in more than 120 years and announced **over $3 billion** in critical-minerals and battery projects, plus **$180 million** in mining-school/workforce investments. The deals target China's chokehold on rare earths, permanent magnets, and battery materials — the "do magnets" structural policy theme. The **Department of War** (formerly Defense) is the lead investor, signaling these are defense-industrial priorities.

**Named projects & amounts:**
- **Department of War — $1.4B → Sila Nanotechnologies**: expand silicon-carbon battery anodes + build a Li-ion cell plant for satellites, drones, and munitions
- **Department of War — $400M → Sunrise Energy Metals**: world's first primary **scandium** mine (high-heat aluminum alloys for fighter jets/spacecraft)
- **Department of War — $150M → Niron Magnetics**: 100% domestic **rare-earth-free permanent magnets**
- **Department of War — $85M → Standard Bauxite**: refractory-grade bauxite for high-temp defense components
- **EXIM — $25M → Westwater Resources**: Coosa Graphite deposit (battery anodes)
- **EXIM — $25M → Global Advanced Materials**: tantalum/niobium (magnets, electronics, steel)
- **EXIM — $8M → 5E Advanced Materials**: boron (magnets, semiconductors, glass)
- **DFC — $4.8M → Harena Rare Earths**: rare-earth mine in Madagascar
- Plus **$100M → 14 mining schools** + $80M to three university tech hubs (workforce)

Since Jan 2025, the administration has signed/approved **160 minerals deals totaling ~$40B**. The market crowned the listed rare-earth/magnet complex: **MP Materials (MP) jumped** and **USA Rare Earth (USAR) surged (+11% on Aug 7, +8.9% still climbing Aug 8)**.
