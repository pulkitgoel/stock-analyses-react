## 🇺🇸 Trump / US Policy Pulse — 24 Jul 2026

### 1. Section 122 Expires, Replaced by Section 301 Forced Labor Tariffs

**What happened:** The 10% Section 122 global import surcharge expired at 12:01 a.m. ET on July 24, 2026 (150-day statutory limit reached). USTR simultaneously finalized Section 301 forced labor tariffs on ~60 economies covering 99.4% of US imports — a two-tier 10%/12.5% structure took effect with no gap. India secured the lower 10% tier after enacting a forced labor import prohibition policy in June 2026. Brazil, Chile, and others face 12.5%. The Section 301 replacement has no statutory time limit or rate cap — this is the durable baseline.

**Exemptions:** Civil aircraft components, specific raw materials, semiconductor equipment, pharmaceutical inputs where US domestic production can't meet demand.
