## 2. What Happened Since 18 June

### 2.1 Q1 FY27 results (reported 27 July 2026) — strong operationally, one miss on the bottom line

| Metric (consolidated) | Q1 FY27 | Q1 FY26 | YoY |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sales | ₹3,281 Cr | ₹2,878 Cr | **+14%** |
| Operating profit | ₹397 Cr | ₹381 Cr | +4% |
| OPM % | **12%** | 13% | -100 bps |
| Other income | ₹84 Cr | ₹28 Cr | +200% |

| Net profit | ₹308 Cr | ₹267 Cr | **+16%** |
| EPS | **₹1.99** | ₹1.76 | +13% |

Standalone (core business, excluding subsidiaries): sales ₹3,061 Cr (+16%), PAT ₹364 Cr (**+27%**), PAT margin 11.9% vs 10.8%, operating margin +140 bps, ROCE 23% for the quarter.

**Segment detail — the divergence is the story:**

| Segment (standalone) | Q1 FY27 sales | YoY | PBIT | PBIT margin |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Power Systems | ₹1,402 Cr | **+31%** | ₹324 Cr | **23.0%** (+209 bps) |
| Industrial Systems | ₹1,671 Cr | +6% | ₹148 Cr | ~8.9% (vs ₹172 Cr PBIT last year) |

Power Systems backlog hit ₹14,434 Cr (+59% YoY). Industrial Systems was dragged by a ₹20 Cr one-off provision in the railways business and weak consolidated industrial margins (7.6% vs 10.2% YoY) — management attributed the gap to G.G. Tronics, which is still pre-revenue and "at breakeven today."

**The miss:** consensus FY27 Q1 EPS was ₹2.20; actual was ₹1.99 — a **-9.7% surprise**, the first negative earnings surprise in four quarters (Q3 FY26 +3.4%, Q4 FY26 +12.9%). The shortfall came from lower Industrial Systems margins and higher semiconductor losses, not from the power business.

**Other operational highlights from the call:**
- Order intake ₹4,692 Cr standalone / ₹5,211 Cr consolidated in the quarter
- Transformer capacity now ~75,000 MVA (power) plus ~10,000 MVA (distribution), up from 22,000 MVA four quarters earlier; the greenfield plant ramps 10,000 → 30,000 → 45,000 MVA across Q1-Q3
- Price hikes of 17.5% plus a further ~5% taken to offset raw material inflation — "the market is absorbing it pretty well"
- Export order intake roughly doubled (+84% YoY); management targets a spread of geographies (utility, renewable, data centre, oil & gas) rather than dependence on any one
- Motors delivered double-digit growth for a fourth consecutive quarter — the turnaround is now a trend, not a quarter
- Cash remains fortress-like: interest cost for the quarter was just ₹4 Cr on ₹3,281 Cr of sales

### 2.2 Statutory auditor change (disclosed 24 July, effective 14 August 2026)

SRBC & Co LLP (EY network) resigned as statutory auditor effective **14 August 2026**, to align with the audit firm of the new holding company — **S.R. Batliboi & Co** (also EY network). Both firms are EY affiliates, and no audit dispute or qualification has been disclosed.

**Why this matters:** the headline word "resignation" is a red flag in Indian markets for good reason, and the stock's volatile reaction in the following weeks reflects that nervousness. But the substance — an EY-firm-to-EY-firm switch to align with the parent — is procedural, not a governance event. We flag it because it is a legitimate reason sentiment wobbled, and because shareholders should note it rather than be surprised by it. Watch for confirmation in the FY27 audit.

### 2.3 CG Semi: G1 OSAT moves from "commissioned" to "commercially producing"

Since June, CG Semi announced **commencement of commercial production at its G1 OSAT facility in Sanand, Gujarat**, and reiterated a **₹7,600 Cr investment plan over five years** across the two facilities (G1 and G2), built with **Renesas (Japan)** and **Stars Microelectronics (Thailand)**. The fabless design arm, Exero Semiconductor (acquired from Renesas for ₹284 Cr), is **already revenue generating**, and management indicated Renesas has committed to offtake a substantial share (~40-50%, per analyst questioning) of output.

This is the transition our June note flagged as the potential 2027 re-rating catalyst: the semiconductor business has moved from construction to revenue, even though it is still loss-making.

### 4.5 Credibility check — what has to be true

- Consensus FY28 EPS of ₹13.93 implies **PAT of ~₹2,190 Cr** — a 35% CAGR from FY26's ₹1,199 Cr, *from a company that just missed quarterly estimates by 9.7%*
- That requires: Power Systems holding 22-23% PBIT margins at roughly double today's revenue; Industrial Systems recovering from 8.9% back toward 12%; and semiconductor losses shrinking rather than widening
- It also implicitly assumes **~20-25% of FY28 profit comes from businesses that barely exist in the P&L today** (OSAT G2 at full ramp)
- To justify today's ₹882 on a more sober **40x exit multiple**, EPS needs to reach ~₹22 — implying roughly FY29-30 on the consensus trajectory. In other words, **you are buying three to four years of compounding in advance**, with the semiconductor option paying for the premium if it works

**Verdict on valuation:** not the "absurd" story the trailing PEG suggested in June, but not comfortable either. It is a high-quality compounder priced for its bull case in the nearer years and its base case beyond FY28.

## 5. What Hasn't Changed

Being honest about the parts of our June bear case that survive intact:

- **Industrial Systems remains the weak engine** — +6% sales, PBIT down to ₹148 Cr, consolidated industrial margin 7.6% vs 10.2%. G.G. Tronics is still pre-revenue. This has not improved in three months.
- **Semiconductor losses still drag the consolidated P&L.** G1 is now producing commercially, but the business is loss-making and G2 is still to come. Every quarter of drag is a quarter where consolidated growth (16%) lags standalone growth (27%).
- **Other income is doing real work** — ₹84 Cr in Q1 FY27 against PBT of ₹423 Cr (20%). Strip it out and the core P/E is higher still. It is legitimate income from the QIP corpus, but it is not operational.
- **FIIs are still net sellers** — 12.69% (Jun'25) → 11.97% (Jun'26), a seventh consecutive quarter of decline. DIIs (14.22% → 18.23%) are absorbing the supply, which is constructive, but the foreign bid has not returned.
- **Execution load is unchanged and heavy** — 1,10,000 MVA transformer target, switchgear greenfield, OSAT G1 ramp plus G2 build, railway approvals. Four simultaneous large programmes.
- **The absolute valuation is still high** on every historical metric: 109x trailing, 63x FY28, 45x FY28 EV/EBITDA, 1.3% earnings yield.
