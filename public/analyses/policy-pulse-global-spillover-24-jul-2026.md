### 3. Section 122 Expiry — Global Importers Get Relief

**What happened:** The 10% Section 122 tariff expired worldwide. For most non-EU, non-USMCA economies (including China, Vietnam, Thailand, Japan, Korea, India, etc.), the replacement Section 301 forced labor tariffs kick in at 10-12.5%. For countries NOT on the 60-economy list, MFN rates resume. EU goods already moved to the 15% trade deal ceiling on Jul 1, so they're insulated. USMCA Canada/Mexico goods enter at 0%. China goods face existing Section 301 lists (7.5-100%).
