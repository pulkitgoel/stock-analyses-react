## Trump Disclosed 517 Trades in August — Including Up to USD 25 Million of Meta

President Trump's **August financial disclosure**, released on **Wednesday, October 7**, shows **517 purchases and sales** worth roughly **USD 74.3 million to USD 273.3 million**, according to CNBC's analysis of the 18-page filing. Purchases accounted for at least **USD 44.2 million** and sales at least **USD 30.1 million**. Trading has slowed from June and July, when he disclosed **more than 1,000 transactions in each month**, but the portfolio remains highly active.

- The single largest transaction was an **August 21 purchase of between USD 5 million and USD 25 million of Meta Platforms**. On the same day the accounts bought **USD 1-5 million each of AT&T, ConocoPhillips, Abbott Laboratories, Netflix and Chevron**.
- Also on August 21, the accounts **sold USD 1-5 million each of Advanced Micro Devices and Church & Dwight**, plus **USD 500,001-1,000,000 positions in Boeing, Home Depot, T-Mobile, Datadog, Dell Technologies, Palo Alto Networks and Nvidia**.
- On **August 10** the portfolio bought **USD 1-5 million each of Microsoft, McDonald's and Comcast**.
- On **August 18**, the accounts bought **USD 1-5 million of senior unsecured notes issued by SpaceX**, carrying a **5.35% coupon and maturing July 2031**. **Two days later** Trump signed a **national space transportation policy** calling for US support for **more than 1,000 launches and reentries annually by 2030**, directing agencies to facilitate commercial access to federal launch facilities. SpaceX is a major Pentagon contractor and NASA launch provider.
- On **August 26**, the portfolio bought **USD 15,001-50,000 of Tyson Foods** on the same day Trump signed a proclamation **temporarily expanding low-tariff imports of lean beef trimmings by 300,000 metric tonnes**, effective September 1 for up to 90 days. The filing does not show whether the Tyson trade preceded or followed the signing.
- The accounts continued buying **municipal debt**, now a portfolio of **as much as USD 1 billion** across state and local governments, schools, hospitals and housing authorities.
- The White House said Trump **does not direct the trades**. Spokesman Davis Ingle said the portfolio is independently managed through discretionary accounts and computer-based models with **"no ability"** for Trump or his family to influence decisions. The 2025 annual disclosure showed **more than 21,000 securities trades across eight accounts holding at least USD 858 million**.

**Why this is a market signal, not an ethics footnote:** the trades are a live, dated record of what the sitting president's accounts are buying and selling — and the **SpaceX notes purchased two days before a policy that expands the commercial launch market** is the sharpest example this year of disclosure timing overlapping policy timing.

## Trump Accounts Finished Auto-Enrolment — Nearly 70 Million Accounts, USD 4.5 Billion In

The White House marked the completion of **automatic enrolment into Trump Accounts** on **October 7**, with Trump celebrating alongside **Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent, IRS CEO Frank Bisignano and philanthropists Michael and Susan Dell**.

- **Nearly 70 million accounts** now exist, **more than 60 million through automatic enrolment**. Every American child under 18 with a valid Social Security number has an account in their name.
- **USD 4.5 billion has been deposited since the July 4 launch**: **USD 1.3 billion in USD 1,000 seed contributions**, **over USD 600 million in family and friend contributions**, and **USD 2.6 billion in philanthropic gifts**.
- Contribution limits: **family and friends up to USD 5,000 a year**, **employers up to USD 2,500 a year** inside the same overall USD 5,000 annual cap. **More than 70 companies** have committed to employer contributions.
- An estimated **80% of accounts belong to families earning under USD 200,000**.

## The De Minimis Exemption Is Suspended Indefinitely — and the Compliance Deadline Is October 22

Customs and Border Protection's rule **indefinitely suspending the de minimis exemption** for low-value shipments hits its **delayed compliance date on October 22** for consignments subject to partner-government-agency data requirements or Chapter 98/99 duties. The practical effect, as AP reported on **October 8**, is **new fees, registration requirements and bond guarantees on low-value international mail** — everything from **textiles and toys to medications**.

- The rule does not single out Canada, but roughly **2.3 million Americans buy their drugs abroad** (2020 JAMA Network study), and mail-order Canadian pharmacies are the most exposed channel.
- A three-month supply of **Eliquis** — **180 5-mg tablets** — costs about **USD 565** from a mail-order Canadian pharmacy versus roughly **USD 1,017** from Amazon's pharmacy arm and **USD 1,029** from GoodRx, per a study by the Campaign for Personal Prescription Importation.
- **Three Florida Republican congressmen** and **more than 70 Democrats** have written to the administration asking for assurances that personal-use medicines will still clear. The White House says Trump **"remains dedicated to lowering the cost of prescription drugs"**, citing most-favoured-nation deals and the **TrumpRx** discounted-drug site; federal data showed US prescription drug prices **down 3.1% year-on-year** this summer.
