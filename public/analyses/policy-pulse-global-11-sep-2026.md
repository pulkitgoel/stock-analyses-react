- **Brent settled at USD 107.63 on September 10 (+5.9%), the highest close since May 19**, after trading above USD 107 intraday. It slipped about 1.5% on September 11 to around **USD 104-106.5** after a USD 6 surge the previous session.
- **WTI closed at USD 102.48 (+6.7%)**, and was near **USD 99-101** on September 11. **US diesel pushed above USD 6 a gallon to a fresh record.**
- **Brent is up more than 8% this week and 10% on some measures**, with the US-Iran war in its seventh month, attacks on tankers in the Strait of Hormuz intensifying, and **Iran-aligned Houthis threatening Saudi oil exports through the Red Sea**.

## BRICS Opens in New Delhi: Payment Rails, Not a Currency

Leaders of the now **11-member** bloc meet at Bharat Mandapam on **September 12-13** under India's chairship, with **Putin, Xi Jinping, Pezeshkian, Ramaphosa and Prabowo** in Delhi and a **New Delhi Declaration** closing the summit. The theme is *"Building for Resilience, Innovation, Cooperation and Sustainability"*, and the agenda covers **food and energy security, healthcare, disaster resilience, trade, investment, technology and critical supply chains**. **Critical minerals are an explicit item.**

Two points of discipline for anyone trading the headline risk:

1. **The summit is building payment plumbing, not a BRICS currency.** India's concrete proposal is to **link member central bank digital currencies (CBDCs) for cross-border settlement**, first proposed by the RBI in January, alongside greater use of national currencies. **Rupee-ruble mechanisms already cover about 96% of India-Russia trade** (Sberbank India). India prefers its own digital rupee over China's mBridge, and it has consistently resisted binding bloc agreements — so expect connectivity announcements, not a dollar-killer.
2. **The de-dollarisation that matters is slow:** lower cost and lower sanctions exposure for moving money between member economies, not a replacement of the dollar's pricing or reserve role.
