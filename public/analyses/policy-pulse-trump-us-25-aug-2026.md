### 2. US-Canada Trade War Escalates
After trade talks collapsed Friday night, Trump imposed sweeping tariffs on Canadian goods:

**Section 338 Tariffs (50% rate):**
- Affects ~$20 billion of Canadian exports (5.5% of total)
- Targets: alcohol, dairy, plywood, wigs, hockey sticks, natural honey
- First use of Section 338 since the 1930s

**New Auto Tariff Threat:**
- Trump threatens 50% tariffs on all cars, trucks, auto parts, and steel from Jan 1, 2027
- Would compound existing 25% auto tariffs already in place
- Canada is America's second-largest trading partner ($715B annual trade)

**Canadian Response:**
- Trade Minister Dominic LeBlanc confirmed Canada will announce "dollar-for-dollar" retaliation tariffs Tuesday (Aug 26)
- Retaliatory tariffs take effect September 8
- Canada exploring trade diversification (new deals with Indonesia, UAE, Philippines, Thailand, India, Mercosur)
