## SEBI Study: 88.5% of Young Traders Lost Money in F&O in FY26

SEBI released a comprehensive study on August 20, 2026, revealing alarming statistics about retail participation in equity derivatives. The study analyzed FY26 trading data and found that 88.5% of individual traders below age 30 incurred losses in futures and options trading.

**Key Findings:**
- Traders under 30 account for 43% of individual trader base
- 73% of individual traders have annual income below ₹5 lakh
- Low-income traders' trading intensity: 75 times their portfolio value
- B30 cities (beyond top 30 financial centers): 67% of derivatives traders, 58% of losses
- Maharashtra, Gujarat, Uttar Pradesh contributed 41% of total net losses
- Individual participation in equity derivatives declined sharply in FY26
- Aggregate net losses declined but average loss per trader increased

**Regulatory Implications:**
- SEBI may tighten F&O regulations, margin requirements, or lot sizes
- Brokerage revenue from F&O could face pressure
- Exchanges may see lower derivatives volumes
