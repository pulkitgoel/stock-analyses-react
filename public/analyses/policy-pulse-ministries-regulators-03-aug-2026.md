### 📉 India Manufacturing PMI — Near-5-Year Low (53.5 in July)

**What happened:** HSBC India Manufacturing PMI fell to 53.5 in July from 54.2 in June — the lowest since Aug 2021. Sales, input purchases and job creation slowed (hiring weakest in 29 months) even as exports stayed resilient. Weaker domestic demand is the drag. This macro softening supports the RBI's easing bias and argues for liquidity support over rate hikes.

### 🏛️ SEBI Open-Market Buyback Amendments — Effective Aug 1

**What happened:** SEBI's buyback amendment regulations took effect Aug 1, restoring **open-market share buybacks through the stock exchange** (unavailable since Apr 2025), capped at <15% of paid-up capital & free reserves, with optional merchant-banker appointment and shortened timelines. This is a structural positive for cash-rich companies seeking capital return and share-price support.
