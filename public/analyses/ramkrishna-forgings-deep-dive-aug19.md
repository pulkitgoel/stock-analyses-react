## 2. Business Overview

### Company Profile
Ramkrishna Forgings Ltd (incorporated 1981) is India's second-largest forging company, supplying critical components to automotive, railways, energy, and construction sectors. The company operates 15 manufacturing facilities across India, Mexico, and Europe.

### Business Segments

| Segment | Revenue Contribution | Key Products |
|---------|---------------------|--------------|
| **Automotive (CV)** | ~55% | Crankshafts, axles, steering knuckles |
| **Non-Auto** | ~35% | Energy storage, railways, mining |
| **Railways** | ~7% | Bogie frames, wheels (new foray) |
| **Exports** | ~40% | North America, Europe, Mexico |

### Key Customers
- Tata Motors, Ashok Leyland, Mahindra & Mahindra
- Daimler, Volvo, PACCAR (International)
- Indian Railways (emerging)

### Recent Capacity Additions
- **56,000-tonne forging press line** commissioned
- **Chennai Wheel Plant:** ₹2,000 Cr investment for forged wheels (production starts next month)
- **Mexico facility:** Serving North American markets
