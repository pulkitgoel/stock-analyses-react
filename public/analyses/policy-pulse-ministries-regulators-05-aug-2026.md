### 🏛️ RBI MPC — Repo Held at 5.25%, Neutral Stance; GDP & Inflation Forecasts Updated

**What happened:** The MPC voted **unanimously to hold the repo rate at 5.25%** (SDF 5.00%, MSF/Bank Rate 5.50%) and **retained the neutral stance**. Growth was **raised to 6.7%** for FY27 (Q1 7.0%, Q2 6.4%, Q3 6.5%, Q4 6.8%; Q1 28 one-year-out 7.3%). FY27 **CPI inflation was lowered to ~5.0%** (Q2 4.7%, Q3 5.9%, Q4 5.5%, core 4.3%) — noting June CPI at 4.4% (above target and first breach in 16 months) on food/fuel. Governor Malhotra flagged uneven-monsoon/El Niño, volatile crude and global trade as key risks but underscored resilient domestic demand and investment. **New: UCB licensing and interest-rate regulation proposals**, and a **polymer currency-note target for early FY28**. Also: **no early withdrawal of the FCNR(B) scheme**; FX reserves "comfortable."

### 🏛️ SEBI Consultation — Depository Receipts for REITs & Listed InvITs

**What happened:** SEBI (Dept of Debt & Hybrid Securities, POD II) issued a **consultation paper on Aug 4, 2026** — based on the Hybrid Securities Advisory Committee (HYSAC) recommendations — proposing to let **REITs and publicly listed InvITs issue Depository Receipts (DRs)** to foreign investors on international exchanges, extending the DR Scheme 2014 to trust units. **Comments are invited by Aug 25, 2026.** If finalised, this gives Indian REITs/InvITs a new foreign-currency capital-raising channel and could **boost unit liquidity and foreign participation**.
