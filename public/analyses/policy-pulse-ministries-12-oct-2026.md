## The RBI Takes Three State Oil Companies Out of the Dollar Market — and Taxes FX Hedging

The Reserve Bank of India announced a package of measures in circulars dated **October 10**, operational from **October 12**, that is the most decisive regulatory defence of the rupee since the 2013 taper-tantrum response. Two components matter for listed companies.

- **The RBI will meet the entire daily foreign exchange requirement of Indian Oil Corporation, Bharat Petroleum Corporation and Hindustan Petroleum Corporation from October 12**, taking the three state-run oil marketing companies' dollar demand out of the spot market. India imports about **85% of its crude**, so these purchases were a constant, predictable source of dollar demand.
- **A Foreign Exchange Risk Reserve (FERR):** authorised dealers must maintain cash with the RBI equal to **20% of the rupee equivalent of the notional value** of each rupee-linked foreign exchange derivative contract **above USD 2 million** used to hedge current-account exposure where the user purchases foreign currency against the rupee.
- Also in the package: a documentation requirement for users hedging contracted exposures, limits on rebooking cancelled contracts, a corresponding reduction for exchange-traded rupee currency derivatives on recognised exchanges, and an extension of ECB and OFCB concessions to **December 31**.

The immediate market effect was the rupee's best session since April: **up 0.99% to 95.77**, after the RBI was seen intervening through dollar sales in both spot and derivatives. The currency is still down about **6-7% this year**, and Governor **Sanjay Malhotra** said last week that it remains undervalued. **Banks are asking the RBI for relief and clarity** on the hedging rules, which raise the carrying cost of a book they had been encouraged to grow.

## September Retail Inflation Hits 5.54%, the Highest Since October 2024

The Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation released September CPI on **October 12**: retail inflation at **5.54%**, up from **4.83%** in August, the highest since October 2024 and well above the RBI's 4% target. The drivers were food costs, with the base effect turning unfavourable — ICRA had projected September crossing 5.0% on exactly that arithmetic.

- Context matters more than the level: the **RBI hiked the repo rate 25 bps to 5.50% on October 7**, moved to a "calibrated tightening" stance, and said it expects CPI to average about **5.8% over the next three quarters**. September is therefore a print that confirms a stance already taken, not one that forces a new one.
- The transmission channel that matters for equities is the front end and the domestic demand chain, not the index level.

## Equity Mutual Fund Inflows Rise 31% and SIPs Print a Record Rs 32,490 Crore

September industry data released on **October 12** showed **equity mutual fund inflows up 31% to Rs 38,306 crore**, with **SIP contributions at a record Rs 32,490 crore** and debt schemes seeing heavy outflows.
