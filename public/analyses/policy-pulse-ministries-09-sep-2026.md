## Record Day: Six Mainboard IPOs Open Together (Sep 9) — First Time in ~30 Years

Six mainboard issues opened for subscription on Wednesday September 9 — collectively seeking **~Rs 4,400 crore** — a single-day cluster last seen on Dalal Street roughly three decades ago. The six: **Rentomojo, ARCIL, Manipal Payment and Identity Solutions, Karamtara Engineering, LCC Projects and Steamhouse India**. Most close on September 11, with listings expected mid-to-late September. The sheer supply (six risk-appetite gauges at once) is itself the market signal — and it is being absorbed into a falling market without disruption.

**Manipal Payment & Identity Solutions (Rs 805 cr; band Rs 322-339; fresh Rs 320 cr + OFS):** subscribed just **0.17x on day 1** (retail 0.73x, NII 0.13x, QIB 0.00x) with GMP ~Rs 33 (+9.7% over the upper band) — early, tepid retail demand consistent with the risk-off tape rather than a weak issue. ARCIL (OFS; band Rs 132-139), Karamtara Engineering (band Rs 241-254), Rentomojo, LCC Projects and Steamhouse India round out the cluster.

## Torrent Gas Files UDRHP-I for a 100% OFS IPO (SEBI, Sep 9)

Torrent Gas Ltd — the Mehta family's city-gas distribution (CGD) arm, with networks across Gujarat, Maharashtra, Rajasthan, UP and Telangana — filed its **Updated Draft Red Herring Prospectus-I with SEBI on September 9**: a **100% offer-for-sale of up to 33.5 crore shares by Torrent Investments**, with no fresh-issue component. The company had filed confidentially in March 2026; SEBI cleared the confidential draft in June, and FY26 profit more than doubled with a strong Q1 FY27. A pure OFS means no new money into the company — the listing will be a valuation event for the promoter holding company and a sentiment/read-across event for the CGD sector's listed multiples (IGL, MGL, GUJGAS, ADANIGAS) at a time when city-gas margins are squeezed by high spot LNG and crude-linked APM pricing.
