## 🌍 Global Markets — KOSPI Back-to-Back Circuit Breakers; Reverse AI Trade; Hormuz Crisis Intensifies; Fed Decision Day

### 1️⃣ KOSPI Triggers Historic Back-to-Back Circuit Breakers

South Korea's KOSPI index crashed for the **second consecutive trading day** on July 29, triggering a market-wide circuit breaker — **the first back-to-back circuit breaker in the exchange's history**. The two-day decline stripped more than **18%** from the index, putting July on course to be the **worst calendar month** in KOSPI history with losses exceeding **30%** — erasing over a year of gains.

**Catalysts:** SK Hynix Q2 earnings (record ₩60.54T operating profit, +557% YoY) **missed consensus** by 6.6%. SK Hynix fell -9.61% intraday, its Nasdaq-listed ADR hit an all-time low of $130.49. Three structural threats converged:
1. **China's CXMT completed Asia's largest 2026 IPO** — memory chip competition
2. **Chinese state-backed DUV lithography mass production** — breaching Western export controls
3. **Nvidia's $250B guarantee to OpenAI** — circular financing fears

**Combined market value destroyed:** ~₩396T ($270B) in Samsung + SK Hynix over 2 days.

### 2️⃣ "Reverse AI Trade" Rotation

As Asian chip stocks crashed, **capital rotated from semis into IT services/software**:
- **Nifty IT +2.55%** on July 29, +16-19% in July
- Infosys +4.50%, TCS surged, HCL Tech rallied
- Jefferies upgraded Indian IT to 'neutral'
- The Nifty IT rise occurred as Korean chip stocks fell -28.9% in July (steeper than 2008 crisis)

**Pattern:** When chips crash on China competition fears, Indian IT services benefit from global rotation.

### 3️⃣ Strait of Hormuz — Iran Rejects Omani Proposal; Oil Surges

Iran rejected Oman's proposal for joint 50-50 oversight of the Strait of Hormuz and made a counterproposal demanding more control. **Oil prices surged +7%** (Brent >$90, US crude $85) after fresh overnight US-Iran fighting, reversing a 14% decline from three sessions.

- **Strait status:** Effectively closed to commercial shipping (~10 ships/day vs 88/day normal)
- **War duration:** ~5 months since late February
- **IMO called for "maximum restraint"** after renewed attacks
- **Risk to markets:** Oil spike complicates Fed's inflation calculus

### 4️⃣ Fed Decision Day (July 29)

The FOMC concluded its July 28-29 meeting today. **Market expectation:** ~64.2% probability of a hold at 3.50-3.75%. But it's described as the closest call in months — a 25bp hike is on the table as oil prices surge and inflation remains stubborn. Dow fell 700+ points ahead of the decision.
