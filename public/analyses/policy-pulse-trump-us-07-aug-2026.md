# Policy Pulse — Trump / US — 07 Aug 2026

## 🇺🇸 Trump Signs Section 232 Tariffs + Price Floors on Polysilicon — First Solar Jumps, US Solar Supply Chain Reshuffles

President Donald Trump on **Aug 6, 2026** signed a Proclamation under **Section 232 of the Trade Expansion Act of 1962** imposing a **15% ad valorem tariff on polysilicon derivatives** (solar wafers, cells, and modules) plus a **minimum import price program on raw polysilicon** and its derivatives, taking aim at China's chokehold on the solar/semiconductor raw material. The remedies take effect **120 days after signing (Dec 4, 2026)**. The White House also authorized the Commerce Secretary to create an **incentive program for building/expanding/refurbishing US polysilicon production facilities** — an explicit onshoring carrot. The rationale: US polysilicon production capacity has collapsed from ~50% of global share in 2005 to **under 2% in 2024**, and polysilicon is a key building block for both solar panels and defense-adjacent semiconductors.

**Why this moves stocks:** This is the clearest "tariff wall protects domestic manufacturer" setup since the pharma Section 232 tariffs. Unlike generic pharma (which was exempted), here the US's single largest advanced solar manufacturer uses a **different technology (thin-film cadmium telluride) that sidesteps conventional crystalline-silicon polysilicon entirely** — making it a structural winner as Chinese supply gets priced up. Markets crowned **First Solar (FSLR)** the clear winner, with the stock surging on the news.

**Key signal:** The **15% derivative tariff + minimum import prices** effectively reprice the entire imported crystalline-silicon solar stack upward, benefiting **US domestic thin-film and module manufacturers** while pressuring import-reliant installers. The **Dec 4, 2026 effective date** gives a 120-day runway for companies (and the market) to reposition — expect FSLR and peers to hold elevated valuations into that date, and watch for **further China export-control retaliation** which could add a supply-chain risk premium. This is a **structural (not headline-driven) BUY for US domestic solar manufacturing**.
