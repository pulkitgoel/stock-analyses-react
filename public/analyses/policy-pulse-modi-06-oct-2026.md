## Cabinet Approves a ₹10,000 Crore SME Growth Fund — Patient Equity With No Compliance Burden

The Union Cabinet, chaired by Prime Minister Modi, approved on **October 6, 2026** the Government of India's **₹10,000 crore commitment to establish the SME Growth Fund (SGF)**, per a Ministry of Finance release posted by PIB Delhi. The fund follows **Para 28 of the Union Budget 2026-27** and is designed to provide **patient growth equity capital to high-potential Small and Medium Enterprises with demonstrated business viability and scalability**.

The detail matters as much as the headline: the PIB text states **no effective date, no fund structure, no eligibility criteria and no application process**. This is a funding approval — a capital commitment — not a scheme with a compliance deadline or penalty. Budget 2026 announced a **₹4,000 crore Self-Reliant Fund** alongside it.

## Cabinet Approves the Integrated Transport and Logistics Authority — One Appraiser for ₹500 Crore-Plus Projects

The Cabinet also approved setting up the **Integrated Transport and Logistics Authority (ITLA) as a Special Purpose Vehicle**, announced by Information & Broadcasting Minister **Ashwini Vaishnaw** (PIB PRID 2319529, 3:07 PM; PMINDIA release 06 Oct 2026). ITLA becomes the **apex body for integrated transport and logistics planning** and will:

- prepare a **long-term National Transport Plan** covering **roads, railways, ports and shipping, civil aviation, inland waterways, coastal shipping, urban mobility and logistics**;
- **evaluate and appraise government infrastructure projects worth ₹500 crore or more**;
- strengthen **research, planning, appraisal and monitoring** across modes and **integrate logistics data**.

The structural point is the one that has never existed in India: a **single authority that appraises projects across rail, road, port, aviation and waterways against one plan**. Today, the same corridor can be appraised by separate ministries with separate cost norms and separate data sets. If ITLA acquires real appraisal authority over the ₹500 crore-plus pipeline, the effect is a shorter pipeline-to-award lag and fewer inter-modal mismatches — which is worth more to EPC and logistics order books than any single contract.

## Ecuador Offers More Crude, Copper and Critical Minerals
