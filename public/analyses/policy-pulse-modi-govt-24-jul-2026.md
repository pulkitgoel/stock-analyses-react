### 1. Cabinet Approves BHAVYA Rasayan Scheme — ₹3,030 Cr for Three Chemical Parks

**What happened:** The Union Cabinet, chaired by PM Modi, approved the Bharat Audyogik Vikas Yojana Rasayan (BHAVYA Rasayan) scheme with a ₹3,030 crore outlay to establish three dedicated chemical parks across India. The scheme runs FY2026-27 to FY2030-31. Parks will be selected through a challenge-based competitive process, each requiring a minimum 8 sq km (2,000 acres) of encumbrance-free land. Centre provides grants up to ₹1,000 cr per park, with states contributing at least ₹500 cr each.

**Shared infrastructure includes:** Common effluent treatment plants (CETPs), treatment/storage/disposal facilities, water supply, solvent recovery, steam generation, interconnected pipelines, and logistics/warehousing. Government estimates shared infrastructure can lower operational costs by 20-30%.

**Why it matters:** India imports ~$80B+ of chemicals annually. This scheme targets import substitution, domestic manufacturing boost, and integration into global chemical value chains. Benefits downstream sectors: agriculture, textiles, pharma, nutraceuticals, construction, auto, electronics.

### 3. India Secures Lower 10% US Forced-Labour Tariff Under Section 301

**What happened:** India secured the lower 10% tariff tier in the new USTR Section 301 forced labor regime, avoiding the 12.5% rate applied to Brazil, Chile, and others. India adopted a forced labor import prohibition policy after the preliminary findings in June 2026, enabling the lower rate. This replaces the now-expired Section 122 10% surcharge.
