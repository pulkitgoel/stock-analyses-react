The inaugural India-Kuwait Joint Defence Committee (JDC) meeting was held in New Delhi on August 27, 2026, marking a significant step forward in bilateral military cooperation between the two nations.

**Strategic Context:**
This first JDC meeting operationalizes the Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) on Defence Cooperation signed during Prime Minister Narendra Modi's visit to Kuwait in December 2024. The MoU established the framework for enhanced defense ties, and this meeting marks the transition from agreement to implementation.

**Defence Diplomacy Momentum:**
The India-Kuwait defence relationship has been gaining traction as part of India's broader West Asia engagement strategy. Kuwait represents an important partner in the region for India—both as a source of energy and as a strategic location for Indian military logistics and cooperation.
