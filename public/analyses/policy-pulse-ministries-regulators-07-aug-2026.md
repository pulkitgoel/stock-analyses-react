# Policy Pulse — Ministries & Regulators — 07 Aug 2026

## 🏛️ SEBI Pushes New "Authorised Persons" Regulatory Framework (NSE Consultation); RBI on Hold; Brokerages Face a Compliance Reshuffle

Two regulator-led threads drive the Indian institutions watch today, layered over a strong PSU-bank-led market close.

### 🔎 SEBI / NSE — Authorised Persons (AP) Regulatory Framework
**NSE issued a consultation paper on Aug 6, 2026** on a proposed **Regulatory Framework for Authorised Persons**, with **comments due by Aug 27, 2026**. The AP model is how stock brokers and NBFCs distribute trading/investment services through a large network of partners (many new-age platforms operate as APs of mainline brokers). Tighter AP rules = a **compliance, KYC, and liability reshuffle** across the broking industry — a structural but graduist change that raises the cost of running AP networks and rewards well-capitalised, fully-compliant incumbents.

**Key signal:** This joins the **SEBI PMS overhaul** and **SEBI Stock Brokers Regulations 2026** (notified Jan 2026) as a recurring **"regulatory compliance uplift"** theme. It is **neutral-to-mildly-negative on margin for mid-sized/AP-heavy brokers**, mildly positive for the **exchanges (BSE)** and well-capitalised incumbents. Confidence is moderate (consultation-stage, forward-looking) — watch the **Aug 27 comment deadline** and final rules.
