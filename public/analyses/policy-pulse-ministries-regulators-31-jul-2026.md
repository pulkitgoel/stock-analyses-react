# Policy Pulse — Ministries & Regulators — 31 Jul 2026

## 💊 India-US Pharma Tariff Resolution — Generic Drugmakers Protected Under Section 232

**Second-half of today's big pharma tariff day.** While the US Section 232 pharma tariffs (up to 100% on patented branded drugs) take effect today in the US, **generics and biosimilars are expressly exempt**. Critically, **India and the US reached a negotiated deal favoring generic drugmakers** — Indian generic pharma exporters gain an exemption and negotiated outcomes in the Section 232 probe, safeguarding pricing and supply.

**What this means for Indian pharma:** The Jul 22 "generic tariff doom" scare (SUNPHARMA, CIPLA, DRREDDY, LUPIN, AUROPHARMA fell 2-2.5% intraday) is now **largely neutralized** — Indian generics face **0%** from Aug 1 under the separate generic-drug tariff regime (2-year grace), and are **exempt** from Section 232. Sun Pharma faces only a **capped 15%** on its innovative products.

## 📋 SEBI (Stock Brokers) Regulations 2026 — New Consolidated Framework

SEBI's new **Stock Brokers Regulations 2026** replace the 1992 framework — a consolidated regime on registration, compliance, governance, net worth, and investor protection for brokers and clearing members. Combined with the **RBI directions effective Jul 1** (bank credit to brokers fully secured, 40% haircut on equity collateral, tighter bank-guarantee rules), the regulatory environment continues to tighten around leveraged retail broking.

**Stock read:** Structurally **negative for volume-sensitive brokers/exchanges** (margin constraints), but the SEBI consolidation provides regulatory clarity for large compliant players.
