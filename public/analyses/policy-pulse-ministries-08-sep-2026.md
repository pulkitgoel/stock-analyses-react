## DAC Clears Rs 1.1 Lakh Crore of Defence Acquisition Proposals (Sep 7) — 98% Domestic

The **Defence Acquisition Council (DAC)**, chaired by Defence Minister Rajnath Singh, on September 7 accorded **Acceptance of Necessity (AoN) for procurement proposals worth ~Rs 1.1 lakh crore** spanning the Army, Navy and Air Force — with **~98% of the value earmarked for domestic industry**, making it one of the largest single-sitting indigenous clearances on record (Kotak Institutional Equities). Unlike the import-heavy March 2026 sitting (S-400, medium transport aircraft), this package is a Buy-Indian / IDDM opportunity.

- **Army:** CBRN recce vehicles, high-mobility vehicles, self-propelled mechanical mine layers, ALH Dhruv advanced light helicopters, trawl tanks, Sarvatra bridge systems.
- **Navy:** Arudhra medium-power radars and **marine gas turbines for warship propulsion** — a pointed import-substitution move.
- **Air Force:** war-fighting enhancements across fighters/transport/helicopters, ground-based multi-purpose jammers, and a tri-service secure-access-card (RFID) system.

Cumulative AoNs since FY24 have now crossed **Rs 18 lakh crore**; Kotak notes AoN is the in-principle first step and RFP-to-contract typically lags ~2 years, estimating **Rs 6.5-7 lakh crore of new orders converting over FY2027-29E**. Markets reacted immediately: **defence stocks rose ~2.5% on Sep 8** with BEL +1.57% (top Nifty gainer) and HAL/Astra Microwave up to +4% intraday.

## SEBI: ETF Trading Framework Goes Live (Sep 7); FPI G-Sec Compliance Eased

Two SEBI items resolved their calendar slots this week:

**(1) ETF trading framework LIVE from Sep 7** — the June 15 circular (deferred one week from Sep 1 after exchange feedback) is now effective: **T-1 closing-price base price, dynamic price bands for most ETFs, a pre-open call auction for commodity ETFs, and revised close-out procedures for liquid/overnight ETFs**. First-day trading showed no dislocation. Impact is structural (cleaner pricing, better arbitrage) rather than event-driven.

**(2) FPI G-Sec-only exemption extended (circular dated Sep 7)** — SEBI removed the **Fully Accessible Route (FAR) condition** from the investor-group exemption, so **all FPIs investing only in government securities** (including General Route investors) no longer need to furnish investor-group details, aligning with RBI's June 5 circular. It lowers compliance friction for debt-only FPIs at a time when India is courting bond-index flows.

## Market Close Sep 8: Sensex -555 to 75,577; Defence Rallies, Banks Drag
