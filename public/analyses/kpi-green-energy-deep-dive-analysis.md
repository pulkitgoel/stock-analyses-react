## 2. Business Overview

KPI Green Energy Ltd (part of the KP Group) develops, builds, owns, manages and maintains renewable power facilities — **solar and wind-solar hybrid** — as an **Independent Power Producer (IPP)** and as a service provider to **Captive Power Producers (CPPs)** under the **'Solarism'** brand.

**Business model — dual engine:**
- **CPP (EPC Model):** Designs & builds renewable power plants for captive customers (Aditya Birla, Adani, others). EPC margins ~16-18%. This is the revenue driver but dilutes overall margin.
- **IPP (Own-and-operate):** Builds and operates its own power assets with long-tenor PPAs. IPP EBITDA margins ~85-90%. This is the **profit engine** that management is deliberately scaling up.

**Group companies:** KP Energy, KP Green Engineering, KPF Green Hydrogen & Ammonia Technology, KP Human Development Foundation.

**Group scale:** KP Group has an installed renewable capacity of **2.5 GW**, targeting **>10 GW by 2030**.

**Portfolio (as of Jun 30, 2026):** **6.94 GW total** = 1.87 GW installed + 5.07 GW under construction; power evacuation capacity 5.10 GW; land bank 8,657 acres.

**Key stat:** Profit CAGR of **104%** over the last 5 years; sales CAGR of **92%** over the same period.

## 15. Bull vs Bear Case

3. **High-margin IPP engine:** As IPP (85-90% EBITDA margin) scales past the capex construction phase, combined margins improve and recurring annuity cash flows grow. Q1 IPP generation was 4x YoY.
4. **Order book visibility:** ~₹5,246 Cr CPP order book + >₹5,000 Cr EPC + fresh platform wins (BESS, floating solar, trading, Botswana).
5. **Undershooting momentum:** The stock is oversold on every oscillator; a short-term technical bounce from the 52W-low zone is plausible.

### Bear Case

1. **Profits are falling NOW:** Q1 PAT -15% (the first meaningful decline), and management admits EPS-accretive only post-FY28. The "growth" is being paid for in the present with suppressed earnings.
2. **Leverage is spiraling:** ₹5,197 Cr borrowings, D/E 1.71, FCF burn of -₹2,608 Cr. A further rate/inflation squeeze or funding hiccup is highly damaging.
3. **Earnings quality risk:** Potential interest capitalization + 4x inventory build + promoter pledge could all mask or amplify downside.
4. **Distribution on results:** The 11-Aug volume spike with only 24% delivery is a textbook distribution signal, not accumulation.
5. **Governance overhang:** Promoter pledges (44.7%) + opportunistic preferential warrant structure drew proxy-advisory criticism; DIIs at just 0.67% shows institutional skepticism.
6. **Stale analyst optimism:** The ₹562 target assumes high growth that Q1 failed to deliver; downgrades are more likely than upgrades near-term.
