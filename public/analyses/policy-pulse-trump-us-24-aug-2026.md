## US-Canada Trade War Escalation

### 50% Tariffs Implemented

Following the collapse of trade talks on August 21, 2026, President Trump imposed **50% tariffs on approximately $20 billion of Canadian imports**, effective August 22, 2026. The tariffs cover:

- Wine and spirits
- Dairy products (milk, cheese, butter, whey)
- Paper products
- Furniture
- Cement
- Clothing
- Fishing rods and hockey equipment

**Notably excluded:** Canadian oil and gas (approximately 60% of US energy imports from Canada remain exempt).

### Canadian Retaliation

Prime Minister Mark Carney announced **"dollar-for-dollar" retaliatory tariffs** on US steel, electronics, and other products, effective **September 8, 2026**. Canada also removed exemptions for US products under the USMCA trade pact.

Carney stated: *"We got attacked. Canada will match Washington's new tariffs dollar for dollar to protect Canadian workers, farmers, families, and businesses."*
