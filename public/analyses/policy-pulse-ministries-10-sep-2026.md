## SEBI Keeps the Closing Auction Session — But Will Rework Derivatives Settlement Pricing

SEBI Chairman **Tuhin Kanta Pandey said on September 10** that the **Closing Auction Session (CAS) is "here to stay"** and that the regulator is only examining whether the derivatives settlement price could be based differently. Speaking at Global Fintech Fest in Mumbai, he noted that **MSCI acknowledged the recent rebalancing went well under CAS**, and that initial liquidity has been a challenge in every jurisdiction that has rolled it out — liquidity builds over time.

The immediate market evidence: on September 10, the **indicative closing levels for the Nifty 50 and Sensex briefly jumped more than 1% in the auction**, against a small loss at the 3:15 pm end of normal trading — a roughly **100-point Nifty and 300-point Sensex adjustment**. The Nifty finished 46.30 points higher at 23,477.80 and the Sensex 138.36 points higher at 74,902.59, both flattered by the auction. CAS has been live since August 3; the flagged problems are divergent closes across exchanges, volatile options pricing (put premiums spiking 400-500% in one recent auction) and manipulation risk on expiry days.

## SEBI Revises Commodity Derivatives Position Limits and Caps Penalties (Circular, September 9)

In a circular dated September 9, SEBI **revised position limits for commodity derivatives** — the existing framework dates to 2017 and was calibrated to market conditions a decade old — and **capped penalties for limit violations**, moving away from open-ended punitive discretion. Both changes reduce the regulatory friction that has kept institutional participation shallow in Indian commodity derivatives.

## NSE's IPO: Price Band Due September 11, Issue Trimmed to About Rs 23,000 Crore, ISIN Frozen

The NSE has **frozen its ISIN (INE721I01024) effective September 11** ahead of listing, the standard pre-IPO procedural step. The **price band is expected to be announced on September 11**, and the offer size has been **trimmed to roughly Rs 23,000 crore** from earlier estimates of about Rs 30,000 crore after changes to some shareholders' offer-for-sale plans. The issue is slated to **open September 18 and list September 25** — which would make it India's largest-ever public issue.

## RBI: Coastal Local Area Bank Brought Into the Second Schedule

The Reserve Bank of India formally **included Coastal Local Area Bank Limited (Vijayawada, Andhra Pradesh) in the Second Schedule of the RBI Act, 1934**, granting it scheduled bank status — which widens its access to RBI liquidity facilities and interbank markets. Separately, **RBI cleared ICICI Prudential AMC to acquire up to 9.95%** of the paid-up capital or voting rights in **AU Small Finance Bank and DCB Bank** (approvals dated September 8), part of a broader ICICI Group clearance across eight banks. The acquisitions must be completed within a year and remain subject to banking and securities regulations.

## Hindustan Zinc Signs a Six-Year Electric-Truck Logistics Contract

**Hindustan Zinc** signed a **six-year transportation contract with MFL India Limited to deploy 30 electric trucks** for concentrate haulage (extendable by two years), progressively replacing diesel vehicles moving zinc and lead concentrate from **Rampura Agucha** to the company's Rajasthan smelters, with MFL India also building dedicated EV charging infrastructure. Hindustan Zinc added 42 EVs to its logistics fleet in FY26 alone and now runs a 232-vehicle green fleet (52 EV, 180 LNG).
