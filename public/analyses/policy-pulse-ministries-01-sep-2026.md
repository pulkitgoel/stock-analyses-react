## SEBI NOC for NSE IPO Confirmed; ETF Trading Framework Deferred to Sep 7; CAS Enforcement Steps Up

### NSE IPO: SEBI NOC Confirmed — September Listing in Focus

- SEBI has issued the no-objection certificate for NSE's long-awaited IPO; NSE CEO publicly thanked the SEBI chairman at FICCI's Annual Capital Markets Conference 2026 (NDTV Profit newsbreak)
- Reports peg valuation at up to Rs 5.26 lakh crore, with an issue size of roughly Rs 30,000 crore (~$3B), and a listing targeted on BSE in September 2026
- This is the exchange's first listing attempt after nearly a decade of regulatory delays; the co-location case settlement is complete
- Global roadshows are expected ahead of the issue

### SEBI Defers ETF Trading Framework to September 7

- SEBI revised the effective date of the ETF trading framework (base price, dynamic price bands, pre-open call auction) from Sep 1 to Sep 7, 2026 — a short deferral, not a rollback
- Dynamic asset-linked price bands replace the flat band system to narrow the gap between ETF market price and NAV

### SEBI Acts on CAS Manipulation (Sensex Expiry)

- SEBI interim order against Copthall Mauritius and Mansi Share and Stock Broking for manipulative trades during the Aug 13, 2026 Closing Auction Session on Sensex expiry; Rs 3.67 crore in alleged wrongful gains impounded
- Signals active oversight of the new CAS mechanism (live since Aug 3 for F&O stocks)
