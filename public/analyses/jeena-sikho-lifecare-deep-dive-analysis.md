## 2. Business Overview

Jeena Sikho Lifecare Ltd (JSLL) is an **integrated Ayurveda healthcare company** — one of India's leading ayurvedic healthcare system providers, operating for over ten years. The company runs a **large-format network of Ayurveda hospitals and clinics** combined with a branded **Ayurveda products portfolio**, both managed on a unified tech-enabled, hub-and-spoke model.

### Core Business Model

| Component | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Hospitals & Clinics** | 119 active centres (61 hospitals + 58 clinics/day-care), across 23 states and 100+ cities/towns |
| **Operational Beds** | ~2,300 beds operational; ~445 more in pipeline; **561 beds added in FY26 alone** |
| **Doctors & Staff** | ~592 certified Ayurveda doctors + ~780 supporting healthcare personnel |
| **Products (SKUs)** | 330+ Ayurveda product SKUs; Q1 FY27 Products segment ₹118 Cr (+47% YoY), GM 86% |
| **Services Segment** | Diagnosis + OPD/IPD care; Q1 FY27 Services ₹106 Cr (47% of mix, GM 91%) |
| **Omnichannel Funnel** | 16M+ social-media followers, health camps, no doctor-referral dependence |

### Segment Mix (Q1 FY27)
- **Services (47%):** ₹106 Cr — Gross Margin 91%
- **Products (53%):** ₹118 Cr — Gross Margin 86%, +47% YoY, 330+ SKUs

### Expansion & Strategy
- **Asset-light unit economics** (management): capex ₹3–4 Lakh per bed (vs ₹30–50 Lakh for allopathy), break-even occupancy ~35%, <6-month payback on smaller hospitals, revenue potential ~₹8,360/day/bed
- New hospitals: Mathura 100-bed, Lucknow 160+ bed, Ahmedabad 150-bed, West Bengal 60+ bed, Jammu, Raigad
- **Ultra-luxury wellness resort** in Mandi, HP (108 rooms, 22 villas, helipad)
- **UAE subsidiary** + 51% UAE Ayurveda acquisition (Jan 2026) — global expansion push
- Partnerships: Salesforce (digital), Chandan Healthcare ₹42.12 Cr diagnostics deal, Niva Bupa & Medsave insurance empanelment, Amazon/Theryco distribution, Sat Kartar Life MoU

### Indices & Status
- Part of **Nifty Smallcap 500, Nifty Microcap 250, Nifty Total Market**
- Member of BSE "B" group; migrated from SME platform (IPO 2022) to main board
