### The Bear Case

- **Inventory days at 117**, up from 86, is where a demand pause would show first.

- **The growth optics get brutal.** A 336% quarter cannot repeat, and next year's comparables sit on a far larger base.
- **Still 70x trailing** for a business with negative operating cash flow.

### Verdict

Netweb is the rare case where the fundamentals caught up with the price. In June the stock sat at 135x earnings with a ₹2,100 Cr order book; today it is 70x, and H1 profit alone has passed the whole of FY26. The QIP bought time on the second; it did not answer it.
