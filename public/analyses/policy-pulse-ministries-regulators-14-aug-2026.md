### 🏛️ SEBI — Accredited Investor Framework Overhaul (Consultation, PR 45/2026, Comments Due Sep 3)
SEBI released a **consultation paper (Aug 13)** proposing a major revamp of the **Accredited Investor (AI) framework**: (1) **manager-led accreditation** alongside the current accreditation-agency route, (2) a new **securities-market-assets eligibility route** (₹5 cr / ₹20 cr), (3) **deemed AI status for non-resident investors**, (4) **longer accreditation validity**. The changes could lift eligible investors to **~4 lakh** and ease access to AIFs and alternative products. This joins the **PMS overhaul, buyback rules, Stock-Broker Regs 2026, Authorised-Persons consultation, and FY27 inspection reset** as a recurring SEBI **"compliance / eligibility re-rating"** theme.

### 🏛️ SEBI — Online Bond Platform Providers (OBPP) Circular (Aug 14, In Force)
A final SEBI circular lets **Online Bond Platform Providers** offer (1) **IFSCA-regulated products** (GIFT-IFSC international instruments, FEMA/LRS-aligned) and (2) **Section 54EC / Section 85 tax-saving bonds** (NHAI, REC, PFC-type issuers), while replacing the mandatory Company-Secretary compliance-officer rule with the **stock-broker NISM certification** standard. This widens the retail debt-investing perimeter — a mild structural tailwind for bond/distribution ecosystems.
