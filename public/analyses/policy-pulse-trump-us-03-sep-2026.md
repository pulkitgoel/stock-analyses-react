## Section 232 Drone Tariffs Go Live: Up to 100% on Imported UAS From Sep 3

Presidential Proclamation 11055 (signed Aug 13, 2026) took effect 12:01 a.m. ET on Sep 3: Section 232 duties on imported unmanned aircraft systems (UAS) and components, following a US Commerce Department investigation into America's reliance on foreign drones (China's DJI dominates the consumer/commercial market).

**Rate structure:**
- Annex I (high-risk/large/thermal-imaging UAS and docking stations): 100%
- Annex II (consumer and commercial UAS under 25 kg - the DJI Mini/Mavic class): 25%
- Allied-country tiers (EU, Japan, Korea, Switzerland, Taiwan, etc.): 10-15% on covered categories
- UAS component duties deferred to Feb 9, 2027 (Annex III)
- Free-trade-zone entry route closed; bonded-warehouse plays for inventory in the certification gap

The market has already voted once on this: US drone makers jumped in pre-market on Aug 14 when the proclamation was signed (Red Cat +7.1%, AeroVironment +3.3%). Now that the duties are actually collecting, the tariff wall is a structural tailwind for US-based drone manufacturing.
