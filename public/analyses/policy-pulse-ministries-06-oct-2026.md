## GST Council Rescheduled Again — Now October 8, Second Deferral

The **57th GST Council meeting has been moved to Thursday, October 8, 2026, at 11:00 a.m. at the Summit Room, Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi**, per GST Council Secretariat **Office Memorandum F.No.1392/57th GSTCM/GSTC/2026 dated October 5**, citing "unavoidable circumstances." This is the **second rescheduling** — it was originally set for September 12 (deferred because of the BRICS Summit), then October 7, now October 8. The Council is chaired by Finance Minister **Nirmala Sitharaman**.

The agenda is **process and enforcement reform, not a rate reset**:

- **Removal of the arrest provisions** under **Section 69 of the CGST Act**, and a requirement of **judicial authorisation** for any arrest.
- **Raising the prosecution threshold from ₹1 crore to ₹5 crore** and narrowing the offences that can trigger criminal proceedings.
- **Faster refunds** — approximately **90% of eligible claims released automatically after a risk check**.
- **Input tax credit protection for genuine buyers** when a supplier defaults (**Section 16(2)(c)**), plus a review of **blocked credits under Section 17(5)**.
- **Easier registration for small e-commerce sellers** — roughly **9.5 lakh** sellers identified.
- More targeted **e-way bill** checks and relief from low-value disputes.
- Possible **input tax credit on group health insurance** — a live industry estimate cited by the Financial Express puts the unlock at **more than ₹5,000 crore** (group health premiums exceeded ₹68,000 crore in FY26; the 18% GST component is roughly ₹12,000 crore).

Context: September GST collections were **₹2.04 lakh crore (+14.7%)** and April–September came in at **₹12.46 lakh crore (+11.6%)**.

## SEBI Moves to Partly Reverse the Closing Auction Session for Derivatives

**Reuters reported on October 5 that SEBI is preparing to partly reverse its newly introduced Closing Auction Session (CAS)** — specifically, to **stop using the auction to set derivatives settlement prices for at least a year and revert to the volume-weighted average price of the final 30 minutes of continuous trading**, while **keeping the auction for closing prices of the underlying shares where the cash market is illiquid**. Changes are reportedly expected by end-October. This is based on unnamed sources; **no revised circular has been verified**, and SEBI's own framing remains a consultation.

The chronology matters:

- CAS went live for F&O stocks on **August 3, 2026**.
- Expiry-day dislocations followed — a **Sensex indicative price fall of about 2,000 points on August 27** and a **2.2% dip in the Nifty's indicative close on September 29**.
- SEBI issued a **seven-proposal consultation paper on September 12**; the comment deadline was **October 3**.
- SEBI corrected press reports of "3,500 comments" — the actual count was **20,000 by 7:00 p.m. on October 3**. FIA strongly opposed immediate adoption of a blended VWAP and backed the CTS-only VWAP as the interim methodology.
