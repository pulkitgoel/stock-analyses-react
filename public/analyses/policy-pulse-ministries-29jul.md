## 🏛️ SEBI PMS Overhaul Consultation Paper — Portfolio Manager Revamp

SEBI has issued a comprehensive consultation paper (Jul 23, 2026) seeking public comments by **August 13, 2026** on a major overhaul of the SEBI (Portfolio Managers) Regulations, 2020. The draft Portfolio Managers Regulations, 2026 propose significant liberalization and structural changes.

### Key Proposals
| Proposal | Detail |
|----------|--------|
| **Overseas Investment** | Allow PMS to invest in specified foreign securities subject to conditions |
| **Unlisted Debt** | Allow limited investment in investment-grade unlisted debt for discretionary PMS |
| **MF-PMS Framework** | New Mutual Fund-only PMS category with reduced entry thresholds |
| **Derivatives Flexibility** | Greater flexibility for exchange-traded derivatives in PMS |
| **Compliance Simplification** | Relaxed dealing room requirements for PMS with <10 clients or AUM <₹100cr |
| **Demat Portability** | Allowing demat account portability for PMS clients |
| **Digital Disclosures** | Enhanced digital disclosure requirements |
