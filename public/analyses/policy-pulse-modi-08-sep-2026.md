## PM Modi Completes the 2,800-km Dedicated Freight Corridor, Unveils Rs 35,000+ Crore Project Pack in Vadodara

Prime Minister Narendra Modi (Vadodara, Gujarat — Sep 8) inaugurated the **final sections of the Western Dedicated Freight Corridor (WDFC)**, marking the **full completion of India's 2,800-km Dedicated Freight Corridor network** — which he called "historic" and a testament to the transformation in the country's work culture over the last 12 years. In the same programme he laid foundation stones, inaugurated and dedicated to the nation **development projects worth more than Rs 35,000 crore**: new railway lines and doubling projects, national highway packages, housing, water-supply and urban-development schemes, plus a **110 MW floating solar plant** in Gujarat.

The milestone matters for markets in two ways: (1) the DFC network now offers a full Western (Dadri-Rewari-Madar) and Eastern corridor backbone, structurally improving rail-freight economics for container operators and wagon lessors; and (2) the Rs 35,000+ crore project pipeline is incremental visibility for rail/road EPC order books. **The PM's announcement names no listed company** — the mappings below are inferences from who executes and benefits from each category (Rule 6 flag: ⚠️ inferred).
