# Policy Pulse — Global — 07 Aug 2026

## 🌍 US Polysilicon Tariffs Reshuffle Global Solar Trade; Crude Stays Two-Way as Hormuz Deal Blurs

Two cross-border threads bind global markets today: the **US Section 232 polysilicon tariff** (a China-facing solar supply-chain reprice) and the **Hormuz deal that is "close but not signed"**, keeping crude in a choppy two-way range.

### 1️⃣ US Polysilicon Section 232 Tariffs — Global Solar Trade Reshuffle
President Trump on **Aug 6, 2026** imposed a **15% tariff on polysilicon derivatives** (wafers, cells, modules) and a **minimum import price program on polysilicon**, effective **Dec 4, 2026**, explicitly targeting China's dominance of the solar/chip raw material. This hits **Chinese polysilicon/solar exporters** hardest (they supply the bulk of the world's solar stack), while **US thin-film maker First Solar (FSLR)** and alternative-technology producers are the confirmed winners.

**Key signal:** Global solar trade is re-segmenting between **China c-Si** (tariffed) and **non-Chinese/alternate-tech supply** (beneficiary). This is a structural rotation, not a one-day headline — expect the tariff wall to hold premium pricing for US/aligned producers through the **Dec 4 effective date**.
