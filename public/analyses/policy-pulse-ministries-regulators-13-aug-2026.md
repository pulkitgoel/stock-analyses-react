### 🏛️ SEBI — BRSR Value-Chain + Reasonable-Assurance Mandate for Top Companies
SEBI is firming up its **ESG/sustainability regime for FY 2026-27**: the **top 250 listed companies** must now file **BRSR Core value-chain disclosures** covering **Tier-1 suppliers** (top upstream/downstream partners ≥2% of purchases/sales by value), with **reasonable assurance** phased in. This is a recurring **regulatory-compliance re-rating** theme alongside the PMS overhaul, buyback rules, Stock-Broker Regs 2026, the Authorised-Persons consultation (comments due Aug 27), and the FY27 inspection reset.
