## PM Inaugurates India Mobile Congress 2026: 6G Leadership, a Global Cyber-Fraud Framework, and USD 15 Billion of BharatNet

Prime Minister Modi inaugurated the **10th India Mobile Congress (IMC) 2026** at **Yashobhoomi, New Delhi on Thursday, October 8**, the four-day event running **October 7-10** under the theme **"Scale Without Boundaries"** with participation from **over 100 countries**. He was received by Communications Minister **Jyotiraditya Scindia**.

**What the Prime Minister said:**

- **5G has reached every district**, with **more than 5.5 lakh 5G base stations** deployed and **mobile internet speeds up tenfold since 2022**, when the rollout began.
- He called for a **global framework to counter cyber fraud and deepfakes**: *"Cybercriminals do not recognise borders. A person sitting in one country can commit a crime in a third country, using resources from a second country."* He asked the **GSMA and telecom operators worldwide** to build a **mechanism that stops fraud before it reaches the people**, and said technology **"can achieve scale only when it gains people's trust."**
- On the next technologies: **6G, artificial intelligence, semiconductor production, cybersecurity and advanced digital infrastructure** are the stated priorities, alongside **domestic manufacturing of mobile phones and telecom equipment**.

**What the Communications Minister put on the record the same session:**

- **India aims to double its 5G user base from 550 million to one billion by 2030.**
- The **Bharat 6G Alliance has 90 institutions involved and has established 7,700 patents, of which 4,400 are international**. Scindia: *"We were behind the world in 4G. We marched with the world on 5G. But it is our resolve that we will lead the world on 6G."*
- The **ITU Council has accepted India's proposal to host the Plenipotentiary Conference in 2030**.
- Broadband customers have grown from **60 million to over 1.1 billion**; India now has **1.35 billion mobile connections**. One gigabyte of data has fallen from **₹268 in 2014 to ₹8** — the cheapest in the world.
- **UPI accounts for 84% of India's digital transactions and 49% of the world's**, processing **260 billion transactions a year worth USD 3.4 trillion**, with the technology taken to **11 other countries**.
- The **USD 15 billion government-funded BharatNet programme** will provide middle-mile connectivity to close to **2.6 lakh gram panchayats** and on-demand connectivity to the remaining **3.8 lakh villages**, with a **100 Mbps connection to every home**.
- The **mobile phone industry has grown from USD 2-3 billion in 2014 to USD 63 billion**; India is the **world's second-largest mobile phone producer** and exports are **close to USD 29 billion**.
