## Modi Opens Rs 13,000 Crore of Tamil Nadu Projects, Led by a Metro Stretch and a Refinery Unit

On **October 11**, at IIT Madras in Chennai and alongside Tamil Nadu Chief Minister **C. Joseph Vijay**, the Prime Minister inaugurated, dedicated and laid foundation stones for projects worth **over Rs 13,000 crore** spanning metro rail, highways, railways, ports, petroleum and research. **PIB names one listed company: Chennai Petroleum Corporation Limited (CPCL).** Every other company mapping below is an inference from the contractor and supplier base, not a named award.

- **CPCL (named):** the Prime Minister laid the foundation stone for the **Group II/III Lube Oil Base Stocks project at CPCL's Manali refinery**, estimated at about **Rs 1,620 crore** with **270 thousand metric tonnes per annum** of capacity, producing base stocks for premium lubricants and reducing lube imports.
- **Metro rail:** the **Poonamallee-Vadapalani stretch of Chennai Metro Phase II**, built at about **Rs 4,900 crore**, was inaugurated — a **3.7-km double-decker structure** with signalling designed to support driverless operations.
- **Highways, about Rs 3,700 crore:** the 36-km four-laned Vadugapatti-Therkuvenganallur section of NH-744, the 30-km four-laned Madurai Ring Road on NH-744A, and the foundation stone for the four-lane Thiruvarur bypass on NH-83.
- **Railways, over Rs 750 crore:** the Karaikal-Peralam line and three rail flyovers around Coimbatore, plus **five redeveloped Amrit Bharat stations** at Palani, Vellore Cantonment, Ambasamudram, Paramakkudi and Namakkal.
- **Ports:** four projects at Chennai Port (a paved storage yard, four EXIM godowns, an upgraded cruise terminal, a seafarers' club), **eight projects at V.O. Chidambaranar Port** in Thoothukudi, the **Coal Berth-3 at Kamarajar Port**, and the foundation stone for capital dredging for Container Terminal Phase-I, Stage-II.
- **Research:** the Sudha Gopalakrishnan Brain Centre at IIT Madras, a mission to map **more than 100 human brains** into cellular-resolution 3D atlases, and the C-STROKE ischaemic-stroke atlas.

The Prime Minister's framing was cumulative rather than project-specific: the metro network has gone from about **250 km in 2014 to over 1,100 km across 25 cities**, against five cities then, and **defence exports have grown fifty-fold**. He also said a decision to invest about **Rs 17,000 crore** has been taken to turn Thoothukudi's VOC Port into a world-class mega container and green-energy hub. **Chief Minister Vijay used the platform to seek early approval for Chennai Metro Phase III** — airport to Kilambakkam, Poonamallee to Sunguvarchatram, and Koyambedu to Pattabiram. That is a request, not an approval.

## One Year of the Dhan-Dhaanya Krishi Yojana — an Anniversary, Not a New Scheme

The **Prime Minister Dhan-Dhaanya Krishi Yojana** marked its first year on **October 11**. To be precise about what is new: **the scheme is not new.** It was approved by the Union Cabinet on **July 16, 2025** for six years from 2025-26, covering **100 districts** selected on low productivity, low cropping intensity and low irrigation. What the anniversary produced is a scorecard: the average score of the 100 districts across six objectives rose from **18.85 in April 2026**.

- The scheme's instruments are conventional — seed and input access, credit, storage and market linkage in the weakest agricultural districts — so the read-through is to input suppliers, mechanisation and irrigation rather than to any single award.
