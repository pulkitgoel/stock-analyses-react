### 1. 🏛️ Section 122 Tariff Expires TOMORROW (Jul 24) — Last Day Countdown

**The 10% global import surcharge expires by operation of law at 12:01 a.m. EDT on July 24, 2026** — tomorrow. The 150-day statutory limit under Section 122 of the Trade Act of 1974 is reached. Key developments:

- **USTR missed its own July 20 deadline** to finalize the proposed 12.5% Section 301 replacement tariffs on 46 countries. No final rule has been published — creating uncertainty about whether there will be a tariff-free gap or an immediate replacement.
- **Sen. Ron Wyden (D-Ore.) introduced the Congressional Trade Powers Reform Act** on Jul 22, which would repeal Section 122 and Section 338 and require congressional approval for Section 301/201/232 tariffs. This signals growing anti-extension momentum in Congress.
- **Bloomberg reports** the Trump administration is racing to patch together replacement tariff authority, with Section 301 (46-country investigation) as the primary vehicle. EU goods are already covered by the Jul 1 EU-US trade deal (15% ceiling). 
- **Brazil's 25% Section 301 tariff** took effect Jul 22 — a separate track from the 46-country action.
- **Section 338 Canada tariffs (50%)** signed Jul 20, effective Aug 19.
