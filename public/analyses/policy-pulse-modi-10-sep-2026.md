## PM Modi at the First International Space Summit: Space Station by 2035, Indian on the Moon by 2040

Prime Minister Narendra Modi addressed the **first International Space Summit in Paris** by video conference on September 10, the second day of the summit, and invited France and other space-faring nations to be "co-travellers in the next phase of India's space research". He described France as a trusted partner in India's space programme since the 1960s.

The concrete targets restated in the address are the investment signal: **an Indian space station by 2035** and **an Indian on the Moon by 2040**, alongside ISRO's cost-effective missions serving farmers, fishermen, weather forecasting, disaster management and navigation. He underlined a commercial fact with export weight — **more than 430 satellites from 34 countries have travelled aboard Indian launch vehicles** — and framed the doctrine as "explore together, innovate together, and protect space together", with scientific data serving the common good.

## Three India-France Space Agreements Signed in Paris — One Names a Listed Indian Company

The summit produced three India-France commercial agreements, announced on September 10:

- **Safran Space (France) with Dhruva Space (India)** — contracts worth more than **EUR 5 million** to supply communication systems, inertial navigation units, optical payloads and ground stations for India's **SBS-3 space-surveillance constellation**: **52 satellites in low-earth and geostationary orbit, 2027-2030**, a strategic project approved by the Cabinet Committee on Security, intended to give the armed forces persistent surveillance, secure communications and Indian Ocean Region monitoring. Safran's release flags follow-on cooperation under future SBS phases.
- **U-Space (France) with AXISCADES Technologies (India, listed)** — a commercial cooperation agreement to address the Indian microsatellite market for both commercial and institutional customers. **This is the one listed-company name in the day's space diplomacy.**
- **RIDE! (France) with TakeMeToSpace (India)** — launch services for two satellites for an Indian orbital-data-centre venture.

The wider summit featured **51 announcements worth roughly EUR 20 billion** in contracts, investments and commitments.
