### 🏛️ SEBI Closing Auction Session (CAS) — Live From Aug 3

**What happened:** SEBI's Closing Auction Session came into effect **Aug 3, 2026** for all F&O-eligible cash stocks (including the entire Nifty 50 and Bank Nifty). Instead of determining the closing price via the **VWAP of the last 30 minutes** (3:00-3:30 PM), the exchange now runs a **dedicated blind-pool auction between 3:15-3:35 PM** to set a single equilibrium closing price. This changes closing-price price discovery, reduces manipulation/volatility at the close, but adds a new closing-auction session that alters how closing-hour liquidity and momentum traders behave.

### 🏛️ India Raises Windfall Tax on Exports — Effective Aug 3

**What happened:** India **raised the windfall tax on petrol, diesel and aviation-turbine-fuel (ATF) exports**, effective Aug 3. This is a **negative for refinery margins** — it claws back some of the windfall refiners earn from export-parity pricing, particularly while crude is volatile. It hits downstream/refining economics even as the global crude picture stabilizes.

**Key signal:** The windfall tax is a **offsetting counter** to the crude-crash margin relief refiners saw Monday. With crude firming ~1.4% today AND a higher export levy, the net OMC/refiner near-term picture is **neutral-to-negative** despite the Aug 3 crude rally.
