## 2. Business Overview

Siemens Ltd is India's leading industrial manufacturing company and a subsidiary of Siemens AG, Germany. The company operates across four key business segments serving India's infrastructure, manufacturing, and energy sectors.

### Core Business Segments

| Segment | Key Products & Services | Revenue Contribution |
|---------|------------------------|---------------------|
| **Digital Industries** | Factory automation, industrial software, motion control, CNC systems | ~35% |
| **Smart Infrastructure** | Building automation, electrification products, fire safety, security | ~30% |
| **Mobility** | Railway signaling, rolling stock, rail automation, turnkey projects | ~20% |
| **Energy** | Power generation, transmission, distribution (being demerged) | ~15% |

### Key Competitive Strengths

- **Technology Leadership:** Access to Siemens AG's global R&D and cutting-edge technology
- **Diversified Portfolio:** Presence across automation, infrastructure, mobility, and energy
- **Strong Order Book:** ₹45,033 Cr backlog provides multi-year revenue visibility
- **Execution Excellence:** Track record of delivering large-scale infrastructure projects
- **After-sales Service:** Recurring revenue from maintenance and service contracts

### Strategic Focus Areas

1. **Factory Automation:** Industrial IoT, digital twins, smart manufacturing
2. **Railway Modernization:** Metro projects, high-speed rail, signaling systems
3. **Smart Cities:** Building automation, energy-efficient solutions
4. **Energy Transition:** Grid modernization, renewable integration (pre-demerger)
