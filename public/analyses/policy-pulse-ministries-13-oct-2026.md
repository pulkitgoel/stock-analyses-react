## The FM Puts LTCG and STT on the Budget Table, and the Market Adds Rs 4.3 Lakh Crore

Finance Minister **Nirmala Sitharaman** said on **October 13** that the government would be "keen to hear views" on the **removal of long-term capital gains tax**, and framed the **securities transaction tax** as part of the same question.

- "LTCG, a lot of people are commenting on, both Indian and foreign investors. This debate on STT in market and question of treating all asset classes similarly, there is a lot of debate. **It is healthy to have this debate.**"
- She stopped well short of promising anything: "We are very close to the Budget and the preparation for the Budget has started, and **it may not be the best time for me to say what I will or what I will not consider**."
- A **senior government official** said the same day that the Centre **has received proposals to review LTCG on equity and is examining them**.
- The push came from **SBI Research**, which on October 12 proposed **abolishing LTCG on equity** and extending the qualifying holding period to two or three years to anchor "hot money" flows. FPIs have sold **Rs 44,166 crore** of Indian equities in October and about **Rs 3.04 lakh crore** in 2026 to date.
- What is actually on the books: **12.5% LTCG on listed equity and equity mutual funds held over 12 months, with Rs 1.25 lakh of annual gains exempt** (July 2024 Budget). Budget 2026 left that structure intact but **raised STT** — futures to 0.05% from 0.02%, options premiums to 0.15% from 0.1%, and exercised options to 0.15% from 0.125%.
- **This is a consultation, not an announcement.** No rate has been changed, and the FM declined to indicate a direction.

## The Army Takes Delivery of 450 Loitering Munitions — the Order Was Placed in June 2025

The Army has inducted **450 Nagastra-1R loitering munitions**, its largest single batch of the "kamikaze" drones, per the **New Indian Express** on October 13.

- The **Rs 158 crore order was placed in June 2025** under the emergency procurement (EP) powers granted after Operation Sindoor. It is a **follow-on to the earlier EP contract for 480 Nagastra-1** units, inducted through 2024 — the first 120 delivered to the Pulgaon ammunition depot in June that year, with the full lot in by December.
- The manufacturer is Nagpur-based **Solar Defence and Aerospace Ltd (SDAL)**, formerly **Economic Explosives Ltd** — a **wholly owned subsidiary of listed Solar Industries India (SOLARINDS)**.
- The armed forces have signed roughly **Rs 30,000 crore** of emergency procurement contracts, largely for drones, counter-drone systems, radars and electronic warfare equipment.
- **Read this as an induction, not as order inflow.** The award was booked in FY26; what changed on October 13 is that the capability is in service.

## The MoD Signals a 10-15 Year Capability Roadmap and a Rewrite of Acquisition Rules

**A. Anbarasu**, Additional Secretary and Director General (Acquisition) at the Ministry of Defence, set out the direction on **October 13**:

- A revised **Integrated Capability Development Plan (ICDP)** will lead to a detailed **Technology Perspective and Capability Roadmap (TPCR)**, giving industry a **10 to 15 year outlook** on military requirements — explicitly including emerging domains such as **drones and counter-drone systems**.
- A **centre for drone excellence at IIT Kanpur** is to be established as a single research location for the domain.
- The central element of the proposed **Defence Acquisition Procedure (DAP) 2026** reforms is a shift **from manufacturing products domestically to India developing and owning critical technologies**.

## NSE Ends Polycab's Derivatives Contracts, and the Stock Falls 5%

The National Stock Exchange said, in a circular dated **October 12**, that it will **not introduce contracts for new expiry months in Polycab India** after the existing contract months expire.

- October, November and December 2026 contracts stay available until their respective expiries. New strike prices will still be introduced in the existing months. **The final contract expires on December 30, 2026**, and all positions must be squared off by that maturity.
- The background: an insolvency case alleging **Rs 2.79 crore** of operational dues, in which **NCLAT has stayed** the earlier NCLT order admitting insolvency proceedings. Admission to the IBC framework or NCLT proceedings is among the disqualifiers under SEBI and NSE eligibility criteria, alongside the revised **Rs 1,500 crore** market-wide position limit threshold.
- A stock removed from the F&O segment **cannot be reintroduced for 12 months**, and must then satisfy the eligibility criteria for six consecutive months before the exchange's Risk Management Committee will verify it.
- **Polycab shares fell more than 5% on October 13.**
