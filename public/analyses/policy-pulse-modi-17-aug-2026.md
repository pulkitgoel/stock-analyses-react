# Policy Pulse — Modi / India Govt — 17 Aug 2026

## 🇮🇳 PM Modi's Independence Day Address (Aug 15) Puts AI, Defence Self-Reliance, Nuclear & Semiconductors at the Centre of "Viksit Bharat 2047"

After a quiet mid-August lull, PM Modi's **80th Independence Day address from the Red Fort (Saturday, Aug 15)** delivered a dense, stock-relevant policy agenda anchored on strategic self-reliance. The speech — his 13th consecutive I-Day address — laid out a **"Viksit Bharat 2047"** blueprint with concrete, sector-wide initiatives that map directly to listed equities. This is the first market session (Monday, Aug 17) reacting to the speech, so it is fresh, unpriced policy signal.

**Key announcements with stock linkage:**

1. **AI skilling for 1 crore youth over the next year** — PM said India "will work to provide AI skill training to one crore youth" to lead in the AI era, alongside leadership pushes in **quantum computing, space tech, robotics and data centres**. This is a direct demand catalyst for India's IT services and edtech/upskilling complex.
2. **Defence manufacturing / self-reliance push** — reiterated India's goal of becoming a global hub for defence manufacturing, stressing **hypersonic systems, drones, and counter-drone platforms**, and noted **work on "Sudarshan Chakra" (the indigenous AI-enabled multi-layered air & missile defence network) is progressing rapidly**. Direct tailwind for the indigenous-defence complex.
3. **Nuclear energy expansion** — placed nuclear energy at the heart of the "developed India by 2047" energy build-out, reinforcing the civilian-nuclear capex theme.
4. **Semiconductor growth** — reaffirmed the long-term push (already anchored by Semicon 2.0's ₹1.27 lakh cr), with 5-8 new semiconductor plants flagged over 7-8 years.
5. **Energy self-reliance** — warned of "weaponisation of resources and sea routes" and urged energy independence, a pointed nod given the ongoing Hormuz crisis (import-substitution capex tailwind for upstream O&G, refineries, renewables).
6. **"Saptadhara" / seven sectors** — manufacturing, food processing, technology, innovation and digital technologies as the growth pillars.
