# Policy Pulse — Trump/US — 31 Jul 2026

## 💊 Section 232 Pharmaceutical Tariffs Take Effect Today — Branded Drugs at 100%, Generics Exempt

The **July 31, 2026** deadline hit today for Trump's Pharmaceutical Section 232 tariffs, imposed under the Trade Expansion Act of 1962. The tariffs apply additional duties of up to **100% on patented brand-name pharmaceuticals and their active ingredients (APIs)** imported into the United States.

**The key nuance — this is NOT a blanket pharma tariff:**

- **17 named companies (Annex III)** — mostly the large global branded drugmakers — face the 100% duty from **today (Jul 31)**. However, most of these have secured tariff exemptions via "MFN pricing agreements" or the **20% onshoring incentive** — companies that commit to shifting production to the US can reduce the rate to 20%.
- **Generics and biosimilars are EXPRESSLY EXCLUDED** from the Section 232 duties — both in the proclamation and via the India-US negotiated deal that favors generic drugmakers.
- **EU, Japan, Korea** face a tiered **15%** rate; **UK** at 10%.
- A second, broader deadline for **all other importers arrives Sept 29, 2026**.

The "20% onshoring incentive" rewards US manufacturing, a positive for durability of US pharma earnings. The wildcard is the **Sept 29** expansion to all importers.
