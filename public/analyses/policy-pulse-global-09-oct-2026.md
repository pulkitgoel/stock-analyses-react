## Brent Slips to About USD 103 as Trump Rules Out an Iran Strike Before the Midterms

Oil gave back part of Thursday's surge after President Trump said on **Truth Social** on **October 8**: "We will not be attacking Iran at any time prior to the Midterm Elections to be held in the United States on November 3rd." He described talks with Tehran as productive and said the **Strait of Hormuz** remains under US control.

- **Brent fell about 1.3% to roughly USD 102.8-103.0 a barrel** on October 9, after settling **4.1% higher at USD 104.28** on October 8; **WTI slipped about 1.2% to around USD 90.4** (prior settle USD 91.49). Brent remained set for a weekly gain.
- **Hurricane Isaias** forced US Gulf producers to shut in about **1.3 million barrels a day — 62.9% of current output** — as of October 8, the other leg of the price move.
- Iran's **Foreign Minister Abbas Araqchi** said Tehran is reviewing the US response to its proposal to **reopen the Strait within seven days**. Attacks persist: the UK Maritime Organization counted at least **nine attacks in the Strait of Hormuz and the Persian Gulf in the first seven days of October**, half of September's total.

## A Strong 30-Year Auction Pulls the Long End Back From a 24-Year High

The US Treasury's **USD 22 billion 30-year auction** cleared at a high yield just under **5.62%**, pulling the **30-year down to about 5.60-5.62% from 5.73%** — a notable reversal after the long bond touched its highest since 2002 earlier in the week.

- The **10-year eased to about 5.23-5.26%** from an October 8 high of **5.35%** (and a 24-year peak of about **5.36%** on October 7); the **2-year was around 4.78%**. The curve flattened.
- Fed speakers stayed hawkish: **Governor Christopher Waller** said further hikes are likely needed to reach the 2% target, though "not necessarily" immediately; **St. Louis Fed President Alberto Musalem** said another increase may be warranted. **Markets price roughly an 80% chance of a hold at the October 27-28 FOMC**, and about **83% odds of a hike by December**.
- September's **jobs report** showed only **+29,000 payrolls** against about **90,000 expected**, with **unemployment at 4.2%**. **US September CPI lands October 14**.

## US-China: the 30-for-30 Lists Are Out, and the Truce Runs to January 10, 2027

The **US-China Board of Trade**, operationalised at Xi Jinping's September Washington visit, published matched lists of about **USD 30 billion of "non-sensitive" goods on each side** — roughly **USD 60 billion** of bilateral trade.

- **US export list (China's cuts): 1,619 product categories** — agricultural goods, fish and seafood, logs and wood products, cosmetics and medical devices. **US import list (US cuts): 77 categories** — toys, small appliances, holiday decorations, kitchenware and children's car seats.
- The broader tariff and export-control **truce was extended two months, from November 10 to January 10, 2027**; the headline rates are unchanged at **30% on Chinese goods and 10% on US goods**. **China committed to import at least 10 million metric tonnes of US coal annually in 2027 and 2028.**
- **Semiconductors and export controls remain outside the framework.** Note the split clock: China's **rare-earth export-control pause expires November 10**, six weeks before the tariff truce.
