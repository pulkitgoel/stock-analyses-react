## Jobs Report Blowout Flips the Script: September Hike Odds Jump to 63%

The August employment report (released 8:30 a.m. ET Friday) came in far hotter than expected: **nonfarm payrolls +162,000 vs ~53,000 consensus** (estimates ranged 45,000-55,000), unemployment steady at 4.1%, average hourly earnings +0.3% m/m and +3.1% y/y. The prior two months were revised up by a combined +55,000 — July flipped from -23,000 to +21,000, so the US has now posted six straight months of payroll gains (heaviest since March). Leisure and hospitality rebounded (+59,000 in food/drinking places) and manufacturing added jobs.

Gold fell ~2% and Bitcoin -2.9% to ~$79,710, with crypto-related stocks down ~5% — all repricing a more hawkish Sep 15-16 FOMC. The offsetting nuance from Fed officials: the jobs number matters less than next week's August CPI/PPI prints, which land before the decision.
