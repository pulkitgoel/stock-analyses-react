## 🏛️ Ministries & Regulators Policy Pulse — 27 Jul 2026

### 1. Supreme Court Denies IEX Interim Relief in Market Coupling Case

**What happened:** The Supreme Court has denied interim relief to the Indian Energy Exchange (IEX) in the ongoing market coupling case. This is a significant regulatory setback for IEX. Market coupling — where power exchanges are forced to operate under a single price discovery mechanism — would fundamentally change IEX's business model and reduce its market share advantage. The denial of interim relief means the case proceeds without protective status for IEX's current market structure.

**Stock Impact:**

|-------|--------|--------|
| IEX | 🔴 SELL | Supreme Court denial of interim relief is a structural negative for IEX's business model. Market coupling would eliminate IEX's competitive moat over other exchanges. Volume-sensitive revenue model faces existential risk. |
