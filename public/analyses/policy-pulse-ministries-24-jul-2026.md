## 🏛️ Ministries & Regulators Policy Pulse — 24 Jul 2026

### 1. SEBI Proposes Sweeping PMS Overhaul — Foreign Investments, Unhedged Shorts, MF-Only PMS

**What happened:** SEBI released a consultation paper on the draft SEBI (Portfolio Managers) Regulations, 2026 with these key proposals:
- **Foreign securities:** Portfolio managers can now invest client funds in listed foreign equity shares, listed foreign debt, and overseas mutual funds/unit trusts (currently prohibited)
- **Derivatives flexibility:** Total exposure up to 1.25x client AUM; unhedged short exposure through equity derivatives up to 50% of client AUM
- **To-be-listed securities:** Permitted investments
- **Unlisted debt:** Up to 10% of AUM in investment-grade unlisted debt
- **MF-only PMS:** New framework with lower entry barriers for investors
- **Simplified compliance:** Consolidation of regulatory provisions, simplified language

**Why it matters:** This is a comprehensive modernization of the PMS regime. For HNIs, it opens overseas investment through a regulated channel. For PMS providers (MOTILALOFS, etc.), it expands product offerings and AUM potential. For exchanges (BSE), higher derivative volumes. For brokerages (ANGELONE, ZERODHA), increased PMS-linked trading flows. For AMCs (HDFCAMC, NIPPONLIFE), the MF-only PMS framework creates a new distribution channel.

### 2. HCL Tech Announces ₹14,257 Cr AI Data Centre in Odisha

**What happened:** HCLTech signed an MoU with the Odisha government to set up a ₹14,257 crore global technology centre in Bhubaneswar — the company's first AI data centre in the upcoming Odisha Sovereign AI Park, in partnership with Sarvam AI (in which HCLTech invested $150M for 10.46% stake in June 2026). The centre will house 5,000 people and is expected to start operations by 2028.
