# Policy Pulse — Modi / India — 07 Aug 2026

## 🇮🇳 Cabinet Approves GOBARdhan — ₹23,731 Crore Compressed Biogas Scheme; City Gas Distributors & Bioenergy Supply Chain Are the Beneficiaries

The **Union Cabinet, chaired by PM Narendra Modi, on Aug 6, 2026 approved GOBARdhan**, the National Circular Bioenergy Scheme for Compressed Biogas (CBG), with a total outlay of **₹23,731 crore**, implemented over **FY 2026-27 to FY 2035-36** and administered by the **Ministry of Petroleum & Natural Gas**. The scheme consolidates prior CBG initiatives (SATAT, MDA, BAM, pipeline, bioenergy CFA) into one integrated platform to drive a **~10x increase in domestic CBG production**.

**Key policy levers:**
- **Assured CBG Offtake:** Procurement by **City Gas Distribution (CGD) entities** to meet a notified **CBG Obligation of 3% in FY27, 4% in FY28, and 5% from FY29 onward** in the CNG (transport) and PNG (domestic) segments — converting the blending mandate into a long-term demand signal.
- **Stable Pricing:** A government-backed administered **CBG price of ₹2,110/MMBTU** with a minimum **10-year horizon** — giving producers long-term revenue visibility.
- **Pipeline connectivity + access to finance + capital assistance** across the value chain.

**Why this moves stocks:** CGD entities are both the **mandated buyers of CBG** (blending obligation) and the natural owners/operators of CBG capacity — they get a guaranteed feedstock-transition pathway and a rising blending revenue line. Biogas/energy-equipment makers and state refiners that co-invest in CBG plants also participate. This is a durable, multi-year clean-energy capex theme.

**Key signal:** GOBARdhan converts India's CBG sector from a patchwork of schemes into a **guaranteed-demand, price-stabilized industry** — the single strongest clean-fuel policy lever for the gas distribution complex in years. The **CGD players (IGL, MGL, GUJGAS, ADANIGAS) are the primary structural beneficiaries** as mandated offtakers; **refiners (IOC, BPCL, HPCL)** participate via co-invested capacity. Watch for subsequent **CBG plant tenders and project awards** as the execution catalyst. This complements the earlier floating-solar (PM-SSY) and offshore-oil (Samudra Manthan) energy-capex themes.
