## US-Canada Trade War: Full Impact Analysis

### Tariff Structure

**US Measures (Effective August 22, 2026):**
- **Rate:** 50% ad valorem
- **Coverage:** ~$20 billion of Canadian exports
- **Key sectors:** Wine, dairy, paper, furniture, cement, clothing, sporting goods
- **Exemptions:** Canadian oil and gas (strategically excluded)

**Canadian Retaliation (Effective September 8, 2026):**
- **Rate:** Dollar-for-dollar matching
- **Coverage:** US steel, electronics, agricultural products
- **USMCA exemptions:** Lifted for affected categories

### Economic Context

The breakdown follows three days of intensive negotiations that collapsed on August 21. U.S. Trade Representative Jamieson Greer stated the US offered Canada "the most preferential treatment of any trading partner," including elimination of 10% softwood lumber tariffs and reduction of 25% auto tariffs, but Canada rejected terms on truck exemptions and French cultural subsidies.

**Trade volume at risk:** $650+ billion annual bilateral trade
