## Leader Stock Trades: Trump's Oil & Gas Trading During Iran War

President Trump's investment accounts have actively traded oil and natural gas stocks while he oversees the U.S. war with Iran, according to financial disclosure filings through Q2 2026 reported by CBS News on August 27, 2026.

### Key Trading Activity

**Notable ExxonMobil Sale:**
On April 7, 2026 — the same day Trump announced a conditional two-week ceasefire with Iran — his accounts sold between $500,000 and $1 million of ExxonMobil (XOM) stock. XOM closed at $163.91 that day and opened down 6.5% at $153.52 on April 8, allowing the trade to avoid the ceasefire-related drop.

- Additional Q1 2026 purchases of up to $3.6 million in oil stocks

**Trading Volume:**

### Conflict of Interest Concerns

The White House maintains that Trump's portfolio is "independently managed by third-party financial institutions" through "computer-based model portfolios" and that neither Trump nor his family can influence investment decisions. However, ethics watchdogs note that Trump declined to place assets in a blind trust, unlike predecessors George W. Bush (blind trust), Barack Obama (index funds only), and Joe Biden (no individual stocks).

The war has driven oil company profits to $125 billion in H1 2026, with ExxonMobil posting $14.5 billion Q2 profits and Chevron at $12 billion — figures significantly higher than the prior year due to crude price surges from Middle East instability.
