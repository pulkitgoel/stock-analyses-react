# Policy Pulse — August 19-20, 2026

## 🇺🇸 Trump Pauses 50% Canada Tariffs (Section 338)

**What happened:** Hours before 50% tariffs on $20 billion of Canadian goods were set to take effect (Aug 19), Trump announced a 3-day pause until August 22, citing a "very fair deal" with Canada. The tariffs under Section 338 of the 1930 Tariff Act covered autos, alcohol, dairy, and other goods.

|-------|--------|--------|
| **F (Ford)** | 🟢 BUY | Auto supply chain relief; Canada is key auto parts supplier |
