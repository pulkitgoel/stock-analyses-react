## India Mobile Congress Opens at Yashobhoomi — the PM Inaugurates Tomorrow, and Starlink Says India Is Ready

The **10th India Mobile Congress (IMC) 2026** opened on **Wednesday, October 7** at **Yashobhoomi, Dwarka, New Delhi**, running to **October 10** under the theme **"Scale Without Boundaries."** It is organised by the **Department of Telecommunications and the Cellular Operators Association of India (COAI)**.

- **Prime Minister Narendra Modi inaugurates the event formally on October 8.** Ahead of it, the PM **interacted with board members of the GSMA**, telling them technology **"must become a tool for inclusive and sustainable development."** Communications Minister **Jyotiraditya Scindia** reviewed preparations at Yashobhoomi.
- **Day 1 (today)** opened with keynotes from **GSMA, SpaceX (Lauren Dreyer, VP Starlink Business Operations), Ericsson and TIM**, followed by the **3rd International Bharat 6G Symposium** with the DoT, **Bharti Airtel, Reliance Jio, Vodafone Idea, Ericsson, Nokia, Qualcomm and C-DOT**.
- **Starlink said its India launch is ready: 20 gateway sites built and user data to remain in the country.** That is the single most commercially consequential statement of the day for the incumbent telcos.
- **Scale:** over **1.5 lakh attendees**, **500+ exhibitors and partners**, **600 startups**, **900 thought leaders**, **2,000+ technology use cases**, 120 sessions and **700 investor meetings**. Day 2 keynotes include **Akash Ambani (Reliance Jio), Sunil Bharti Mittal (Bharti Enterprises) and Kumar Mangalam Birla (Aditya Birla Group)**, with government announcements, partnerships and investment discussions expected on the day the PM attends.
- **Focus tracks:** 6G, AI-native telecom, 5G monetisation, satellite communications, semiconductors, quantum, cybersecurity, robotics, edge/cloud and network APIs.

## Modi and Switzerland's Parmelin Close the State Visit With the India–EFTA Prosperity Summit

## Rural Employment: 375 Permissible Works Notified, a 41% Expansion

The **Ministry of Rural Development notified the Schedule of Permissible Works under the Viksit Bharat – Guarantee for Rozgar and Ajeevika Mission (Gramin) Act, 2025 (VB–G RAM G)** on **October 6** — **375 permissible works** against **266 under MGNREGA**, a **41% expansion**. PIB and News On Air carried it as a headline government item on October 7.

- **Break-up:** 128 works under **Water Security**, 111 under **Core Rural Infrastructure**, 98 under **Livelihood-related Infrastructure** and 38 under **Special Works for Extreme Weather Events and Disaster Preparedness**.
- **181 works are agriculture and allied**, including 52 individual-beneficiary works; 107 are tagged for **Antyodaya/vulnerable households**; 36 each for **women and youth**.
- **Livelihood infrastructure is now 26% of the work list against 1.1%** under the previous framework; new categories include agro-processing, grading/packaging and cold storage, mini flour mills, market yards, dairy infrastructure, poly-houses, aquaculture structures and SHG worksheds/SHE Marts.
- **Money:** central allocation of **₹95,692.31 crore for 2026-27**, with the government putting the **total programme outlay above ₹1.51 lakh crore** including states; **60:40 Centre-state funding** (90:10 for north-eastern, Himalayan states and Jammu & Kashmir); **125 days of guaranteed wage employment** per rural household.
- **Demand:** about **15.64 million households sought work in September 2026, up nearly 34% year-on-year** under the predecessor framework.
