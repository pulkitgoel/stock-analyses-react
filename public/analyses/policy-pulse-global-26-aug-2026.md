The escalating US-Canada trade conflict has moved beyond bilateral tensions to threaten North American supply chain integration. Trump's invocation of **Section 338** — imposing 50% tariffs on Canadian autos, dairy, and alcohol — has triggered Canadian retaliation on $20 billion of US goods.

**Global Impact Assessment:**
