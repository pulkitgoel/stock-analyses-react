# Policy Pulse — Trump / US — 18 Aug 2026

## 🇺🇸 Trump Signs Executive Order for 50% Tariffs on Canadian Goods Effective August 19

President Donald Trump signed an executive order on **August 18, 2026**, imposing **50% tariffs** on a wide range of Canadian goods, with the measures scheduled to take effect on **August 19, 2026** — less than 24 hours from signing.

### Legal Mechanism: Section 338 of the Tariff Act of 1930

Trump is invoking **Section 338 of the U.S. Tariff Act of 1930** (the Smoot-Hawley Tariff Act) to justify the duties. This legal framework allows the President to impose tariffs up to 50% if a foreign country places U.S. commerce at a disadvantage, **bypassing standard procedural requirements** including Congressional approval and standard public-comment periods.

### Stated Justification
The administration cites Canada's alleged discrimination against U.S. commerce:
- **Bans on U.S. alcohol** in eight Canadian provinces
- **Restrictions on dairy access** and supply management
- **Caps on vehicle exports** to the U.S. market

### Key Insight

This is the **first use of Section 338 in approximately 100 years** — a significant escalation in trade policy tools beyond the more commonly used Section 232 (national security) and Section 301 (unfair trade). Legal experts note that while importers may challenge the tariffs in U.S. courts, the precedent of previous tariffs being struck down by the U.S. Supreme Court in February 2026 complicates the legal landscape.
