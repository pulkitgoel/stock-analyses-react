## Key Policy Developments

### 1. SEBI F&O Study: Retail Wealth Destruction (August 2026)
SEBI's Department of Economic and Policy Analysis published a devastating study on individual derivatives traders:

**Key Findings:**
- **88% of individual F&O traders lost money** in FY26 (even after SEBI's restrictive measures)
- **₹3.85 lakh crore** (~$40 billion) total losses over 5 years
- **Under-30 traders**: Lost ₹19,287 crore; 88.5% of young traders lost money
- **Behavioral trap**: Traders "chase losses" - 91% reporting losses after 1 year continue trading
- **Leverage danger**: Low-income traders trade at 75x their portfolio value

**SEBI's Regulatory Response:**
- Increased lot sizes to reduce speculation
- Raised margin requirements
- Introduced Closing Auction Session (CAS) for F&O stocks
- Multiple disclosure alerts and nudges implemented

**Market Impact:**
- Active F&O client count fell by 1/5th
- Equity derivatives turnover declining
- Option premium collections expected to drop 15-20%
- F&O orders expected to decline 35%

### 2. PLI 2.0 for Mobiles: Scale Wins
The government's new Mobile Phone Manufacturing Scheme (MPMS) favors companies with manufacturing scale, export capabilities, and domestic supply chains:

**Scheme Structure (₹62,500 crore over 5 years):**
- **Incentive range**: 2.25% to 5.0% depending on thresholds
- **Qualification**: Minimum ₹10,000 crore turnover in FY25-26
- **Incremental targets**: ₹5,000 cr (FY27) → ₹25,000 cr (FY31) cumulative
- **Higher tier incentive**: 5.0% for eligible sales above baseline

**Domestic Sourcing Bonus (up to 1.5% additional):**
- Display modules: 0.3%
- Camera modules: 0.3%
- Enclosures: 0.5%
- Batteries (including cells): 0.2%
- USB cables/connectors: 0.2%
*(Requires 25%+ domestic components in units sold)*

**Indian Brand Support:**
- Separate eligibility for India-registered manufacturers
- Minimum ₹1,000 crore turnover for FY26
- 5% on incremental sales + 3% for Indian design/R&D

**Motilal Oswal Analysis:**
> "Dixon Technologies is well positioned to benefit given its brand-level scale. The scheme's scale requirements restrict competition to companies with sufficient manufacturing capacity and backward integration."
