## Supreme Court Disposes SEBI Pleas vs NSE After Rs 1,500-Crore Settlement: IPO Path Cleared

The Supreme Court on Sep 3 disposed of SEBI's appeals against NSE in the co-location and dark-fibre cases after the regulator approved a ~Rs 1,500-crore settlement with the exchange - removing the last major regulatory hurdle to NSE's long-delayed IPO (co-location/dark-fibre litigation had blocked listing for years). Combined with the SEBI NOC received Sep 1 (valuation up to Rs 5.26 lakh crore; issue ~Rs 30,000 crore; BSE listing targeted in the September window), the exchange's listing path is now essentially clear - the remaining steps are DRHP/roadshow timing.
