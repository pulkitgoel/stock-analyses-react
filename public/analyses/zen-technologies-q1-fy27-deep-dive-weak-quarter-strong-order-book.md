## Management Commentary (from Investor Presentation & Pre-Concall Materials)

The earnings conference call is scheduled for today (27 July 2026), but the investor presentation released on 25 July offers significant color:

### On Execution & Revenue Timing

**"We entered FY27 with clear visibility on execution"** — Management stated that Q1 FY27 revenue was **consistent with expectations** as the execution of orders secured from October 2025 onward is weighted towards Q2 and Q3 FY27. The typical execution cycle is ~12 months, and revenue recognition follows a back-ended pattern.

Key quote: *"We remain on track with our delivery schedules. With a materially broadened product suite and a strong order pipeline, we are well placed to convert the opportunities ahead of us as the year progresses."*

### On the Order Book

**Consolidated order book: ₹1,239.02 Cr** as of 30 June 2026, comprising:
- **Equipment orders:** ₹920.59 Cr
- **AMC orders:** ₹318.43 Cr

Already in July 2026, the company received an additional **₹177.50 Cr simulator order** from Ministry of Defence for upgradation and integration of Tank and Crew Gunnery Simulators.

### On Product Portfolio Expansion

Seven new products unveiled including:
1. **AI-powered anti-drone system** (High-altitude man-portable system launched on Kargil Vijay Diwas)
2. **Hyperstrike interceptor drone**
3. **Zen Vrishab unmanned ground vehicle**
4. **Integrated Smart Border Suite** (India's first AI-powered border surveillance solution)
5. R&D completed for **first Air Force training solution**
6. OEM partnerships at **advanced stage for air simulator segment** entry
7. DAC approval for ₹52,000 Cr including AKASH TARANG anti-UAV system

### On the Air Simulator Expansion

A critical strategic update: Zen has completed R&D for its first product offering to the Indian Air Force and is in **advanced-stage negotiations with OEM partners** for entry into the air simulator domain — expected to be formalized during FY27. Currently, the company is the **leader in Army and Navy domains**; Air Force would complete the trifecta.

### On Industry Tailwinds

Management highlighted:
- Government's sustained prioritization of **counter-drone systems, layered air defence, and unmanned platforms**
- **DAC clearances** over past months supporting procurement environment
- Continued emphasis on **Buy Indian-IDDM** (Indigenously Designed, Developed and Manufactured) procurement
- These trends "reinforce the relevance of Zen's portfolio and create opportunities for order conversion"

## Key Board Decisions

1. **QIP Timeline Extension:** The board approved extending the deadline for utilizing unutilized QIP proceeds by **24 months** (from August 23, 2026 to August 22, 2028). The funds are earmarked for inorganic growth, acquisitions, and strategic initiatives. **No change in the objects** of the issue — only the timeline.

2. **New Board Appointments:**
   - Jasthi Krishna Kishore appointed as **Additional Director & Non-Executive Independent Director** (3 years from 25 Jul 2026)
   - Shilpa Choudari reappointed as **Whole-Time Director** for another 3 years from 1 Nov 2026

3. **ESOP Activity:** 70,000 equity shares purchased through ESOP Trust; 5,850 shares transferred to eligible employees.

## Operating Metrics Deep-Dive

### Revenue Analysis

The ₹141.64 Cr revenue is the **lowest quarterly figure in 7 quarters** — below the ₹152-178 Cr range maintained since Dec'24. On a sequential basis, revenue fell 20.5% from Q4 FY26, and the March 2025 quarter had an anomalous ₹324.97 Cr (driven by large-order deliveries in Q4 FY25).

### Margin Analysis

**Operational EBITDA margin of 27.3%** is the weakest in 7 quarters, a far cry from the 38-42% historical range. Causes:

- **Employee costs** rose to 24.3% of revenue (vs 20.1% a year ago) — talent acquisition for expanded order book
- **Other expenses** surged 83.9% YoY to ₹30.05 Cr
- The 40.9% EBITDA figure quoted in the investor presentation **includes Other Income of ₹19.16 Cr** — the operational figure is the 27.3%

### Other Income Reliance — Caution Flag

**Other Income of ₹19.16 Cr constituted 39.4% of PBT** — significantly above the sustainable threshold of 20-25%. While Zen has a strong cash-rich balance sheet, this level of non-operating income dependency is unusual and merits monitoring.

### Exceptional Item

An **exceptional loss of ₹3.37 Cr** was recorded after inventory at subsidiary Unistring Tech Solutions was damaged in a fire on April 25, 2026. The damaged inventory is **adequately insured** — the insurance survey is pending.

5. **Working Capital Intensity:** The ₹419 Cr working capital build in FY25 signals lumpy cash conversion cycles typical of defence contracts.
