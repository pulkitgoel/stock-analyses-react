## PM-DHARA's First Trading Day: the Transformer Complex Leads the Board

**The ₹1,86,405 crore PM-DHARA scheme — the Green Energy Corridor Phase-III intra-state transmission programme plus 50 GWh of battery storage — was cleared by the Union Cabinet chaired by the Prime Minister on September 30, after the Indian market had closed.** Thursday, October 1 was therefore the first session in which the decision could be priced, and it was priced selectively rather than as a sector wave.

The clearest evidence sits in the BSE gainers list for the session, on a day when the Sensex fell **570.59 points (-0.79%)** and the Nifty shed **198.50 points (-0.88%)** with **2,574 stocks declining against 1,023 advancing**:

- **Transformers and Rectifiers (India) closed up 9.29%**, the **single largest gainer on the BSE** — a pure transmission-equipment name, and precisely the product category the intra-state programme consumes.
- **Adani Energy Solutions rose 4.31%**, the third-largest BSE gainer — the listed transmission and distribution platform closest to an intra-state network build-out.
- **Waaree Energies traded flat at ₹2,366**, but its subsidiary **Waaree Power commenced production on a new solar inverter manufacturing line** — capacity added into exactly the segment the programme's generator-end storage and evacuation design pulls demand from.
- The wider complex did not follow: on a weak, rate-fearful tape, the gains were confined to the names with direct product exposure rather than spreading to the diversified capital-goods index.

That is the correct read of a programme like PM-DHARA. It is **a ten-year, ₹1.86 lakh crore order pool, not an earnings event**, and the market's first-day response — one equipment maker up nearly 9.3% while the index fell — is a positioning response rather than a forecast revision. The structural points from the scheme design, carried in this tracker's September 30 edition, still govern: **₹1,36,378 crore for intra-state transmission** and **₹50,000 crore for 50 GWh of storage**, enabling **135 GW of renewable evacuation**, with **Central Financial Assistance of ₹54,082 crore**, greenfield packages awarded by **tariff-based competitive bidding** and brownfield work on a cost-plus basis, **state transmission utilities as implementing agencies**, and completion targeted for **FY2032-33**.

## The PM at the UPSC Centenary: "Nation First, Citizen First", and a 2047 That Is More Urban

**The Prime Minister participated in and addressed the centenary celebrations of the Union Public Service Commission at Bharat Mandapam on Thursday, October 1**, marking 100 years since the Commission's establishment on **October 1, 1926**, and releasing a **commemorative coin, a commemorative postage stamp and a book, "100 Landmark Judgements — Shaping the Policies of UPSC and Public Service Commissions in India."** The two-day programme opened on September 30 with "Shatabdi Samaroh — Manthan."

The speech is worth extracting precisely, because three of its threads have economic read-throughs:

- **"Nation First, Citizen First" as the two touchstones for selection.** The Prime Minister said the recruitment examination must not only test what a candidate knows but also **why that young person wants to enter the civil services** — an explicit call for service motive over office motive. He called on the Commission to **aggressively upgrade its operations while fiercely protecting its foundational credibility**, and said its core identity — **independence, impartiality, merit, confidentiality and fair processes — must remain eternally uncompromised.**
- **A 2047 forecast with infrastructure consequences.** "By 2047 India will be very different from today's India. India will be **more urban** than it is today. **The gap between villages and cities will narrow.** And the aspirations and expectations of our citizens, especially our youth, will be far greater."
- **Administrative capacity as the delivery constraint.** The PM framed the Commission's second century around the state's ability to execute, in a vein consistent with the government's stated AI-skilling target of training one crore people.

The urbanisation line is the tradeable one, and it is a **speech inference, not a scheme announcement** — the Cabinet's ₹1,790 crore Intelligent Traffic Management System for Delhi, approved in the same September 30 sitting as PM-DHARA, is the concrete companion decision. Read together, the government's stated model is a country that urbanises faster than it builds, which keeps urban infrastructure, water, cement and mobility demand structurally bid. Note also that the market's urban complex did not rally on the day: the Nifty Realty index gained 1-2% while the broader tape fell, in line with the PSU and private bank strength that carried the session.

## Captain Smit Machchhar, and India's Aviation-Security Moment

**The Prime Minister opened his UPSC address by asking the country to pray for Captain Smit Machchhar's long life and good health, and to acknowledge him as a brother who has raised India's pride. President Droupadi Murmu joined the praise.** The context is the September 30 incident aboard **flydubai flight FZ1073, Dubai to Tel Aviv**, carrying **174 people**: the captain, an Indian national, was **stabbed by his co-pilot in an alleged attempt to crash the aircraft**, and — despite being seriously injured — he **released the cockpit door from his seat**, allowing the crew and passengers to overpower the attacker. The aircraft diverted safely to **Tabuk, Saudi Arabia**. Machchhar is recovering in hospital in Tabuk; the Israeli prime minister's office has said it will **ramp up security screening of pilots flying into Israel**.

Within India the staffing-screening thread has its own recent precedents: an **Air India first officer was removed from a Zurich-Delhi flight in late September after failing a breath analyser test**, weeks after another pilot was dismissed following a positive test for a psychoactive substance. And on October 1, **Tewolde Gebremariam assumed charge as Air India's chief executive**, outlining cost discipline, profitability, safety and efficiency as his priorities.

## The Calendar That Actually Sets the Tape Now

The Prime Minister's legislative agenda is largely executed for this window — the Cabinet has cleared PM-DHARA, the Rabi price policy and the Delhi traffic system. What governs Indian equities from here is a dense three-week policy calendar, and two thirds of it belongs to regulators rather than the government:

- **October 3** — SEBI's public-comment window closes on the seven-proposal **Closing Auction Session** consultation. The board meets on **September 24's** proposals with a follow-up; CAS is the single biggest market-structure change of the year for expiry-day volumes.
- **October 5-7** — **RBI Monetary Policy Committee**, with the decision on **October 7**. A hike is genuinely in play. Bandhan Life sees **50-75 basis points**; economists surveyed expect **25bp in October and 25bp in December**, taking the repo from **5.25% to 5.75%**; Brickwork Ratings calls a **25bp move to 5.50%** a real possibility — which would be the **first increase since early 2023**. The drivers are the rupee near **96**, Brent back above **$100** on the December contract, the US 10-year at **5.27-5.34%**, record foreign outflows and the **$133 billion** FCNR(B) inflow deluge that has left the RBI absorbing liquidity through variable-rate reverse repo auctions.
- **October 7** — the **57th GST Council**, rescheduled from September 12, with ITC and supplier-default rules, corporate-guarantee valuation under Rule 28(2), registration simplification and the GST Appellate Tribunal on the agenda.
- **October 13-15** — the UPI MDR regime returns to the Supreme Court two days before **the 0.4% merchant discount rate goes live on October 15** for person-to-merchant transactions above ₹2,000. **Gold traders have announced a protest on October 15** against the MDR applying to jewellery transactions.
- **October 19** — the **India-New Zealand free trade agreement enters into force.**
- **December** — the **G20 Leaders' Summit in Florida** closes out the US presidency of the forum that Piyush Goyal is attending in Milwaukee this week.
