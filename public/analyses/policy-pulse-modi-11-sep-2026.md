## Modi Opens the BRICS Business Forum with a Trade-Barrier Mandate — Summit Adopts the New Delhi Declaration Sunday

Prime Minister Narendra Modi opened India's BRICS chairship climax at the **BRICS Business Forum in New Delhi on September 11**, telling the **BRICS Business Council to identify the top 10 trade barriers** facing member economies and to **support 100 startups annually** across the bloc. His framing was explicit: **trade barriers are rising globally and India is building economic bridges** rather than walls — a direct contrast with the tariff environment India itself now faces from the United States.

The **18th BRICS Summit runs September 12-13 at Bharat Mandapam** under the theme *"Building for Resilience, Innovation, Cooperation and Sustainability"*, and closes with the **New Delhi Declaration**. This is India's fourth chairship; more than **350 meetings across 25 cities** were held during it. The bloc is now **11 members** (Brazil, Russia, India, China, South Africa, Egypt, Ethiopia, Indonesia, Iran, Saudi Arabia, UAE) with **Xi Jinping, Vladimir Putin, Masoud Pezeshkian, Cyril Ramaphosa and Prabowo Subianto** in Delhi. **Critical minerals are an explicit summit agenda item** per Minister G. Kishan Reddy.

For Indian equities the trade is in **market plumbing rather than a currency**: trade finance, cross-border settlement and market infrastructure are where Delhi's proposals convert into earnings.

## The Modi-Putin Bilateral: Energy, Nuclear, Fertilisers, Critical Minerals, Railways and Steel

President Putin arrived in New Delhi early on September 11 and held **bilateral talks with PM Modi within hours** — their **second meeting in two weeks** after Bishkek on August 31. The agenda covered **civil nuclear energy, fertilisers, defence, critical minerals, manufacturing, railways and steel**, with media reporting the defence discussion includes **Su-57 fighters, S-400 systems and BrahMos upgrades**.

Two hard numbers define the relationship. Russia's share of **India's crude imports hit a record 51% in July** as Middle East disruptions pushed refiners toward discounted Russian barrels, and the two sides have a **USD 100 billion bilateral trade target by 2030**.

## HAL's Russian Aircraft Manufacturing MoU "Expanded" — IL-114 and Sukhoi Superjet 100

Civil Aviation Minister **Ram Mohan Naidu confirmed on September 10** that India and Russia have **expanded talks on manufacturing aircraft in India under HAL's existing MoU with Russia** — specifically the **turboprop IL-114** and the **Sukhoi Superjet 100** jet. His stated rationale is regional connectivity: *"with a lot of airports coming up, it is very important for us to connect them… we want to start the process as soon as possible of getting their planes manufactured in the country."*

This is the cleanest stock-level signal in the India file today, because **HAL is named**. Everything else in the aviation-supply chain is inference.

## India Market Context — Fifth Straight Weekly Loss as Oil and Yields Bite

The Sensex closed at **74,781.76 (-120.83, -0.16%)** and the Nifty at **23,398.10 (-79.70, -0.34%)** on September 11, a **fifth consecutive weekly loss**, after an intraday slide of 742 points on the Sensex. **Fourteen of 16 major sectors fell on the week**; mid-caps lost 1.4% and small-caps 0.9%. **Realty was the weakest sector (~-2.7%)**, with Godrej Properties -6.62% and Cochin Shipyard -9.17% among mid-cap laggards, while **metals, auto and pharma each fell about 0.5%**. **IT closed green (+0.11%)** and **Bank Nifty +0.24%**, led by **HDFC Bank (+2.08%)** after winning all seven Bahrain Credit Suisse AT-1 cases, with Dr Reddy's (+1.97%) and Tech Mahindra (+1.00%) next. **Hindalco (-3.21%), JSW Steel (-2.99%) and Tata Steel (-2.02%)** led losses.
