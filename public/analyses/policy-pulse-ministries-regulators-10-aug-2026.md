# Policy Pulse — Ministries & Regulators — 10 Aug 2026

## 🏛️ SEBI Resets the Broker-Inspection Playbook: Risk-Based Supervision, Joint Inspections, and a 1/3 Cut in FY27 Inspection Targets — a Compliance-Tailwind for Well-Capitalised Incumbents

**SEBI (Aug 7, 2026, PR 44/2026)** streamlined its inspection framework for market intermediaries — **effective FY 2026-27**. Under the revamped, **risk-based model** (replacing the old frequency-based approach):

- **Joint inspections** by SEBI + stock exchanges for stock brokers and Depository Participants (DPs), and SEBI + the Supervisory Body for Investment Advisers (IAs)/Research Analysts (RAs)
- **Inspection target cut to ~one-third** of the prior fiscal's level
- **Low-risk / compliant intermediaries** get **reduced inspection frequency**; **high-risk / repeat-alert** entities get more scrutiny

This is a **structural easing of regulatory cost** for the well-run, low-risk end of the broking stack — a mild but real positive for **compliant large brokers and exchanges**, and a wedge that raises the relative cost of doing business for smaller, higher-risk intermediaries.

**Key signal:** Joins the SEBI **PMS overhaul**, **Stock Brokers Regs 2026**, and the **Authorised Persons consultation** (comments due Aug 27) as a recurring **"regulatory compliance re-rating"** theme. Net direction here is **mildly positive for compliant incumbents** (fewer inspections, joint oversight) and neutral-to-negative for higher-risk fringe intermediaries. Confidence moderate (framework begins FY27; targets can be tautened).
